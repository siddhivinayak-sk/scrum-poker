# Requirements Document

## Introduction

The application has two real-time boards: the Scrum Poker session page (`SessionPokerPageComponent`, route `/session/:sessionId`) and the retrospective board page (`RetroBoardPageComponent`, route `/retro/:sessionId`). Participants currently have no way to talk to each other inside the tool, so discussion moves to a separate chat application and loses the context of the round or board.

This feature adds a floating chat to both boards. A floating action button (the Chat_Launcher) opens a floating Chat_Panel in which every Participant of the same Board_Session can read and send short plain-text messages. Messages are scoped to one Board_Session, held in server memory for the life of that session, and replayed to Participants who join late or reconnect.

### Facts from the codebase that shape these requirements

- **Transport.** Each board already holds one WebSocket per tab. The poker board uses `WebSocketService` against the poker `wss`; the retro board uses `RetroWebSocketService` against `retroWss` on the `/retro` path. `server.ts` splits upgrades by path and strips `BASE_PATH` when `PRESERVE_PATH=true`. Chat reuses these two connections and adds no HTTP route and no WebSocket path, so it inherits the existing `BASE_PATH` handling unchanged.
- **Identity.** Both handlers resolve the user from the JWT in the connection URL (`validateToken`) and promote the session owner to `moderator`. That server-held `User` is the only trustworthy sender identity; anything the client puts in a chat payload about authorship is ignored.
- **Session isolation.** Poker and retro have separate registries (`SessionRegistry`, `RetroSessionRegistry`), separate client maps (`sessionClients`, `retroSessionClients`) and separate handlers. AGENTS.md requires these concerns to stay isolated, so each side owns its own Chat_Buffer inside its own session object (`GameSession`, `RetroSession`). Pure, stateless helpers (validation, normalisation, merge rules) may be shared, following the precedent of the `connection-episode` reducer that both client WebSocket services share without sharing state.
- **Precedent for a user-text event.** The Callout_Note feature (`callout:note:submit` / `callout:note:added` / `callout:note:rejected`) already validates the claimed session ID against the connection, trims text, enforces a length limit with `String.length`, and answers rejections on a dedicated event so the generic `error` toast path is untouched. Chat follows the same pattern.
- **Silent send drop.** Both client WebSocket services drop `send()` calls silently while the socket is not `OPEN`. Chat therefore tracks its own Delivery_State and cannot rely on `send()` for failure reporting.
- **Interaction blocker.** While `ConnectionState` is not `connected`, both pages mark their content wrappers `inert`. Chat controls sit outside those wrappers so the chat stays readable during a disconnect.
- **Occupied screen corners.** On the poker page today, the Vote_Callout_Layer (`vote-callout-layer.component.ts`) is fixed at `right: 16px; bottom: 16px` (z-index 900, `flex-direction: column-reverse`, `align-items: flex-end`, 8 px gap, `max-width: min(360px, calc(100vw - 16px))`, `max-height: 40vh`). Below 768 px it adds `left: 16px; right: 16px`, `max-width: calc(100vw - 32px)`, `max-height: 45vh` and `overflow-y: auto`, so it spans the full width. The Moderator_Note_Balloon stack (`moderator-note-balloon.component.ts`) is fixed at `left: 16px; bottom: 16px` (z-index 905, `column-reverse`, 8 px gap, `max-width: min(360px, calc(100vw - 32px))`, `max-height: 50vh`, `overflow-y: auto`). Both layers set `pointer-events: none` and take pointer events only on their controls. Toasts sit top-right (z-index 10000). Dialogs and menus use z-index 1000, the mobile history backdrop 999 and the countdown overlay 9999.
- **Bottom-left arrangement.** The Chat_Launcher takes the bottom-right corner. The Vote_Callout_Layer moves to the bottom-left corner and the Moderator_Note_Balloon stack is stacked directly above it (Requirement 12 criteria 3, 4, 9 and 11). This is a deliberate amendment of the vote-reveal-fun-callouts spec: R7.6 (the anchored edge becomes the bottom-left corner), R7.7 (the 16 px offset is measured from the left and bottom edges) and, on a Narrow_Viewport, R8.1 (the width limit becomes the viewport width less 88 px so the layer clears the Chat_Launcher). R7.1, R7.2, R7.3, R7.4, R7.10, R8.2, R8.7 and R8.8 of that spec stay in force. The `vote-callout.stylesheet.spec.ts` suite asserts `right: 16px; bottom: 16px`, the narrow `left: 16px; right: 16px` and the narrow `max-width: calc(100vw - 32px)` of the layer and of the near-average panel, so those assertions are replaced (Requirement 20 criterion 8).
- **Far-prompt entrance.** The `callout-slide-in` keyframe in `vote-callout.keyframes.scss` starts the Far_From_Average_Prompt at `translateX(calc(100% + var(--callout-edge-gap))) rotate(9deg)`, which is off the right edge only while the layer is anchored to the right. Once the layer is anchored to the left, that start position lies inside the viewport. vote-reveal-fun-callouts R5.2 requires the entrance to start outside the nearest viewport edge, so the keyframe is mirrored to start off the left edge (Requirement 12 criterion 13), and the R5.2 slide-in assertion of `vote-callout.stylesheet.spec.ts` is replaced by its mirrored form (Requirement 20 criterion 8). R5.2 itself stays in force unchanged.
- **Session lifetime.** Poker sessions are deleted through `DELETE /api/sessions/:sessionId` or the inactivity cleanup (0 participants and 30 days idle). Retro sessions are deleted through `DELETE /api/retro/sessions/:sessionId`, which broadcasts `retro:session:ended`, or the inactivity cleanup (0 participants and 30 minutes idle). Holding chat inside the session object means chat disappears with the session.

### WebSocket transport hardening (in scope, Requirements 23 and 25)

These problems exist before chat. Chat makes them more relevant, so this specification fixes them:

1. `server.ts` logs every WebSocket upgrade URL (`WebSocket upgrade request: ${reqUrl}`). That URL carries the JWT in its `token` query parameter, so tokens are written to the server log. No other server log line writes a URL or token today (the registry logs write only session and owner IDs).
2. Both `WebSocketServer` instances (`wss` and `retroWss`, created with `{ noServer: true }`) use the `ws` default `maxPayload` of 100 MiB, so one frame can be very large before any handler validation runs.
3. No server code attaches an `error` listener to an accepted WebSocket. When `ws` rejects a frame (for example one above `maxPayload`), it closes the socket with code 1009 and emits `error` on that WebSocket; with no listener, Node.js treats the event as an uncaught exception, which can stop the whole server process.
4. The largest legitimate client-to-server frame is the poker bulk import (`issue:add` with `titles`). `IssueListPanelComponent.bulkImport()` sends every non-empty line in one frame with no client-side cap, and the issue-list spec states a bound of 200 issues of up to 500 characters (R14.14 of that spec). In the worst case (every character a 3-byte UTF-8 code point, or a 6-byte JSON escape such as `\u0001`) that frame is about 300,000 to 600,000 bytes. Retro card, comment and context text has no server-side length limit but is typed by hand, and every other event of either board is below 4 KiB. The chat send frame is bounded by `CHAT_RAW_TEXT_MAX_LENGTH`. A limit of 64 KiB would reject legitimate bulk imports, so the limit is set to 1 MiB (1,048,576 bytes), which covers the bulk-import bound with margin and is 100 times smaller than the default.
5. Neither client WebSocket service checks frame size, and the shared `connection-episode` reducer treats every close code other than 4009, 4010 and 4004 as an ordinary drop. Because a successful `open` resets the attempt counter, a client that sends an oversized frame after each reconnect would loop through 1009 closes forever.
6. In `WebSocketService` and `RetroWebSocketService`, `openConnection()` calls `close()` on the previous socket and assigns a new one to `this.ws`, but the previous socket's `onopen`, `onmessage` and `onclose` handlers stay attached. When the previous socket's `close` event arrives later, its handler sets `this.ws = null`, which drops the reference to the new socket, and dispatches a `close` to the Connection_Episode_Reducer, which schedules a reconnection and opens a further socket. The same happens when `connect()` follows `disconnect()` before the old close event arrives. Messages carry no socket identity, so a consumer cannot tell which socket delivered them. Requirement 25 makes both services ignore every event of a Stale_Socket and exposes a Connection_Generation. This fix applies to all events of both Board_Kinds, not only to chat events.

### Retrying the deferred chat chunk (Requirement 20)

The Chat_Panel implementation is loaded through a programmatic `import()` (Requirement 20 criterion 4). The Angular 21 application builder bundles with esbuild: it emits the lazily imported code as a hashed file (`chunk-<HASH>.js`) and rewrites the `import('./…')` call in the compiled output to `import("./chunk-<HASH>.js")`, relative to the chunk that holds the call.

A plain retry of the same `import()` is not enough. Under the HTML module-loading rules as implemented in Chromium-based browsers, a failed module fetch is stored in the document's module map under its URL, so a later `import()` of the same URL rejects without a new network request until the page is reloaded ([whatwg/html#6768](https://github.com/whatwg/html/issues/6768)). A spec change to stop caching failed fetches ([whatwg/html#10327](https://github.com/whatwg/html/pull/10327)) has not shipped in every browser ([Chromium issue 534781954](https://issues.chromium.org/issues/534781954)). The module map key is the full URL, query string included, so importing the same file with a different query string makes the browser fetch it again.

The Panel_Chunk_Loader therefore retries with a Cache_Busted_Import of the same emitted file (Requirement 20 criteria 10 to 18):

- The hashed file name is not known in the source code. The loader reads it from the source text of the compiled importer function (`Function.prototype.toString()`), which holds the rewritten specifier, and resolves it against `import.meta.url` of the module that holds the importer. Both are in the same emitted chunk, so the resolved URL is the Chunk_URL, including any `BASE_PATH` prefix. The [`@fatso83/retry-dynamic-import`](https://github.com/fatso83/retry-dynamic-import) library uses the same technique.
- Limitation: a Cache_Busted_Import changes the URL of the Deferred chat chunk only. A file that the chunk imports statically keeps its URL, so a failed fetch of such a file would stay cached. Requirement 20 criterion 4 therefore requires every static import of the Deferred chat chunk to be already loaded by the Board_Page, and Requirement 22 criterion 19 checks this and the readable specifier on every production build.
- Alternative considered: bundling the Chat_Panel into the board page chunks removes the separate fetch and its failure mode. It was not chosen because the cache-busting approach is feasible and keeps the deferral of Requirement 20 criterion 4. It remains the fallback if a future builder version makes the check of Requirement 22 criterion 19 impossible to pass.

### Assumptions

The following values were chosen where the request left them open. Each is a named constant in `shared/types.ts` so it can be changed in one place.

| Constant | Value | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| `CHAT_MESSAGE_MAX_LENGTH` | 500 UTF-16 code units, counted after normalisation | Short chat lines; same counting rule as `NOTE_MAX_LENGTH` |
| `CHAT_RAW_TEXT_MAX_LENGTH` | 2,000 UTF-16 code units, counted before normalisation | Bounds the work done on oversized input before trimming |
| `CHAT_HISTORY_LIMIT` | 200 messages per Board_Session | Bounded memory; enough context for a meeting |
| `CHAT_RATE_LIMIT_COUNT` / `CHAT_RATE_LIMIT_WINDOW_MS` | 5 messages per rolling 5,000 ms per user per Board_Session | Allows quick replies, stops floods |
| `CHAT_ACK_TIMEOUT_MS` | 10,000 ms | Matches the end-session timeout already used by the retro page |
| `CHAT_DEDUP_PER_AUTHOR` | 50 most recent Client_Message_Ids per author per Board_Session | Covers retries even after the message leaves the Chat_Buffer |
| `WS_MAX_PAYLOAD_BYTES` | 1,048,576 bytes (1 MiB) per WebSocket message, for both servers | Covers the largest legitimate frame (bulk issue import, finding 4 above) with margin |
| `WS_PAYLOAD_TOO_LARGE_CLOSE_LIMIT` | 3 consecutive 1009 closes of one Board_Connection | Stops a reconnect loop on repeated oversized frames |
| `CHAT_PANEL_LOAD_RETRY_DELAYS_MS` | `[1000, 2000, 4000]`: 3 automatic retries per Load_Sequence | Bounded backoff; a Load_Sequence ends within about 7 s plus fetch time |
| `CHAT_PANEL_LOAD_ATTEMPT_TIMEOUT_MS` | 10,000 ms per attempt | A stalled fetch counts as a failed attempt; same value as `CHAT_ACK_TIMEOUT_MS` |

Other assumptions:

- Chat is available at any time while the Board_Session exists in its registry, whatever the poker round or voting state, the countdown, the retro phase or the retro `isCompleted` flag (Requirement 1 criteria 11 and 12). Completion locks board content, not the conversation. Chat ends only when the Board_Session is deleted or ended (`DELETE` route, `retro:session:ended`) or removed by the inactivity cleanup.
- Chat messages always show the sender's display name. The retro `showCardAuthor` setting applies to cards only.
- The app has no dark theme today. Chat uses the existing light-theme CSS custom properties in `client/src/styles.scss`.
- The app is English-only. Chat strings are English and are not localised.
- Chat is shown only on `SessionPokerPageComponent` and `RetroBoardPageComponent`. The legacy `PokerPageComponent` is not routed (`/poker` redirects to `/lobby`) and gets no chat.

### Out of scope

The following are not part of this feature. No requirement below may be read as asking for any of them.

1. **Private or direct messages.** Every Chat_Message goes to the whole Board_Session. There are no one-to-one or group-subset conversations.
2. **Attachments.** No file, image or GIF attachments, and no emoji picker.
3. **Editing messages.** A sent Chat_Message cannot be changed.
4. **Deleting messages.** A sent Chat_Message cannot be removed by its author or anyone else. (Discard in Requirement 8 only removes a local `failed` Own_Message that the server never accepted.)
5. **Reactions, threads and @mentions.**
6. **Persistence.** Chat is not saved to a database or to disk, and does not survive a server restart.
7. **Typing indicators.**
8. **Read receipts.** The Read_Marker is local to one tab and is never sent to the server or to other users.
9. **Notifications outside the page.** No desktop notifications, push notifications or sounds. The only new-message indication is inside the page (Requirement 2 and Requirement 24).
10. **Rich content.** No rich text, Markdown, link auto-detection or link previews. Text is shown as plain text.
11. **Cross-session chat.** No chat across Board_Sessions, and no chat between a Poker_Session and a Retro_Session.
12. **Multi-replica sharing.** Chat is not shared across server replicas. The existing in-memory session model already requires a single replica or sticky routing.
13. **Moderator tools.** No muting a user, no removing another user's message, and no session setting that turns chat off.
14. **Chat in exports.** Chat is not included in the estimate export, the retro export or the retro screenshot.
15. **Profanity filtering and content moderation.**
16. **Join and leave system messages** in the Message_List. Presence is shown through the Presence_List instead, because reconnects and the liveness probe would otherwise flood the list.
17. **Tagging and publishing the release.** Creating the git tag `v0.0.9` and publishing the GitHub release are done by the maintainer. Requirement 26 covers only the version changes in the repository.

## Glossary

- **System**: The Scrum Poker application as a whole, spanning the Angular client and the Node.js/Express server.
- **Chat_Feature**: The client and server parts added by this specification.
- **Poker_Session**: One active poker session, held as a `GameSession` in the server `SessionRegistry`.
- **Retro_Session**: One active retrospective session, held as a `RetroSession` in the server `RetroSessionRegistry`.
- **Board_Session**: Either a Poker_Session or a Retro_Session. Every requirement that names Board_Session applies to each kind separately.
- **Active_Board_Session**: A Board_Session that is present in its registry (`SessionRegistry` for poker, `RetroSessionRegistry` for retro). A Board_Session stops being active only when it is deleted through its `DELETE` route (including the retro end-session flow that broadcasts `retro:session:ended`) or removed by the inactivity cleanup.
- **Board_Kind**: The value `poker` or `retro`, identifying which kind of Board_Session a chat element belongs to.
- **Poker_Board_Page**: The `SessionPokerPageComponent` rendered at `/session/:sessionId` while the session-found branch is shown.
- **Retro_Board_Page**: The `RetroBoardPageComponent` rendered at `/retro/:sessionId`.
- **Board_Page**: Either the Poker_Board_Page or the Retro_Board_Page.
- **Board_Page header**: The existing header bar at the top of a Board_Page.
- **Connection-status indicator**: The existing element of a Board_Page that shows the Connection_State to the user.
- **Board_Connection**: The existing WebSocket connection of one browser tab to one Board_Session: the poker `wss` connection managed by `WebSocketService`, or the retro `retroWss` connection on the `/retro` path managed by `RetroWebSocketService`.
- **Client map**: The existing server-side map from Board_Session ID to the open Board_Connections of that Board_Session: `sessionClients` for poker and `retroSessionClients` for retro.
- **Unknown-event path**: The existing `default` branch of the poker and retro WebSocket handlers, which answers an unrecognised event name through `sendError` with code `UNKNOWN_EVENT`.
- **Participant**: A user who holds at least one open Board_Connection registered in the server client map of a Board_Session.
- **Authenticated_Identity**: The `User` record the server derived from the validated JWT when the Board_Connection opened, including the promotion of the session owner to the `moderator` role.
- **Connection_State**: The existing `ConnectionState` value (`connected`, `reconnecting`, `disconnected`) exposed by the client WebSocket service of the Board_Page.
- **Interaction_Blocker**: The existing wrappers on each Board_Page that receive the `inert` attribute while Connection_State is not `connected`.
- **Chat_Launcher**: The floating circular button that opens the Chat_Panel.
- **Unread_Badge**: The count indicator rendered on the Chat_Launcher.
- **Chat_Panel**: The floating panel that holds the Panel_Header, the Message_List and the Composer.
- **Deferred chat chunk**: The lazily loaded JavaScript file, emitted by the Angular application builder, that holds the Chat_Panel implementation (Requirement 20 criteria 4 and 10 to 18).
- **Panel_Chunk_Loader**: The client component that loads the Deferred chat chunk through `import()`, retries failed attempts and reports the loading state to the Chat_Launcher (Requirement 20 criteria 4 and 10 to 18).
- **Load_Sequence**: One run of the Panel_Chunk_Loader started by an activation of the Chat_Launcher or of the "Try again" control: 1 initial attempt followed by at most 3 automatic retries, ending at the first successful attempt or after the last failed attempt.
- **Chunk_URL**: The absolute URL of the emitted Deferred chat chunk, as the browser would request it for the compiled `import()` call, including any `BASE_PATH` prefix.
- **Cache_Busted_Import**: An `import()` of the Chunk_URL with an added query parameter `retry=<n>`, where n makes the URL differ from every URL imported earlier in the document.
- **Chat_Load_Error_Notice**: The element shown next to the Chat_Launcher after a Load_Sequence fails, holding the text "Chat could not be loaded.", a "Try again" control and a "Dismiss" control (Requirement 20 criterion 14).
- **Panel_State**: The display state of the Chat_Panel: `closed` (only the Chat_Launcher is shown), `open` (the full Chat_Panel is shown) or `minimized` (only the Panel_Header is shown, docked above the Chat_Launcher).
- **Panel_Header**: The top bar of the Chat_Panel holding the title, the online count, the Presence_List toggle, the minimize control and the close control.
- **Minimize control**: The control of the Panel_Header, shown while Panel_State is `open`, that sets Panel_State to `minimized`.
- **Restore control**: The control of the minimized Panel_Header that sets Panel_State to `open`, with the fixed accessible name "Restore chat". The restore control covers the title and the Unread_Count area of the Panel_Header and excludes the close control.
- **Close control**: The control of the Panel_Header, shown while Panel_State is `open` or `minimized`, that sets Panel_State to `closed`.
- **Presence_List toggle**: The control of the Panel_Header that shows and hides the Presence_List.
- **Online count**: The text "N online" in the Panel_Header, where N is the number of distinct user IDs in the participant list of the Board_Page.
- **Message_List**: The scrollable region of the Chat_Panel that renders Chat_Messages.
- **Composer**: The multi-line text input and send control at the bottom of the Chat_Panel.
- **Draft**: The text currently held in the Composer and not yet sent.
- **Counter**: The Composer text `<length>/500` shown while the Draft holds 400 or more UTF-16 code units.
- **Rate-limit text**: The Composer text "You're sending messages too fast. Try again in N s." shown after a `CHAT_RATE_LIMITED` rejection.
- **Offline status text**: The Chat_Panel text "Chat is offline until the connection is restored." shown while Connection_State is not `connected`.
- **Chat_Status_Region**: The single element with `role="status"` inside the Chat_Panel, present in the document while Panel_State is `open`, into which the offline status text, the rate-limit text and the not-sent notice of an Own_Message are inserted so that each is announced (Requirement 11 criterion 18).
- **Chat_Message**: One message as defined by the shared `ChatMessage` type, with the fields `id`, `seq`, `clientMessageId`, `authorId`, `authorName`, `text` and `sentAt`.
- **Confirmed Chat_Message**: A Chat_Message in the Chat_Store that the server has accepted and that carries a Sequence_Number, received through an added event, a Chat_History, or as the replacement of an Own_Message.
- **Client_Message_Id**: A UUID generated by the client for one send attempt and reused for every retry of that same message.
- **Sequence_Number**: The `seq` field of a Chat_Message: a positive integer assigned by the server, starting at 1 and increasing by exactly 1 for each Chat_Message accepted in one Board_Session.
- **Own_Message**: A Chat_Message whose `authorId` equals the user ID of the signed-in user of the current tab.
- **Unsequenced_Own_Message**: An Own_Message held in the Chat_Store without a Sequence_Number: one with Delivery_State `pending` or `failed`, or one set to `sent` by a `CHAT_DUPLICATE` rejection under Requirement 8 criterion 3.
- **Chat send event**: The `chat:message:send` event on a poker Board_Connection or the `retro:chat:message:send` event on a retro Board_Connection.
- **Added event**: The `chat:message:added` event for poker or the `retro:chat:message:added` event for retro.
- **Rejected event**: The `chat:message:rejected` event for poker or the `retro:chat:message:rejected` event for retro.
- **Chat_Buffer**: The server-side, in-memory, bounded list of accepted Chat_Messages held inside one `GameSession` or `RetroSession`.
- **Chat_History**: The ordered list of Chat_Messages in the Chat_Buffer at the moment it is sent to a client, delivered on the `chat:history` event for poker or the `retro:chat:history` event for retro.
- **Chat_Validator**: The pure function that normalises raw chat text and classifies it as valid or as one rejection code.
- **Normalised_Text**: The output of the Chat_Validator for an input string: line breaks converted to `\n`, Disallowed_Characters removed, and leading and trailing whitespace removed.
- **Disallowed_Characters**: The code points U+0000 to U+0008, U+000B to U+001F, U+007F, U+202A to U+202E and U+2066 to U+2069 (C0 control characters other than tab and line feed, DEL, and the bidirectional embedding, override and isolate controls).
- **Chat_Rate_Limiter**: The server-side component that counts accepted Chat_Messages per user per Board_Session over a rolling window.
- **Dedup_Record**: The server-side record, per Board_Session, of the most recent Client_Message_Ids accepted from each author, with the Sequence_Number each one produced.
- **Poker_Chat_Handler**: The chat event routing added to `server/src/websocket/handler.ts`.
- **Retro_Chat_Handler**: The chat event routing added to `server/src/websocket/retro-handler.ts`.
- **Chat_Store**: The client-side, signal-based state of the chat for the Board_Session shown in the current tab.
- **Delivery_State**: The client-side state of an Own_Message send attempt: `pending`, `sent` or `failed`.
- **Acknowledgement timer**: The `CHAT_ACK_TIMEOUT_MS` timer of one `pending` Own_Message, which accumulates only time in which Connection_State is `connected` (Requirement 8 criterion 5 and Requirement 9 criterion 3).
- **Read_Marker**: The highest Sequence_Number the current tab has shown to the user while the Chat_Panel was `open` and the document was visible, stored in `sessionStorage` under a key built from Board_Kind and Board_Session ID.
- **Unread_Count**: The number of Chat_Messages in the Chat_Store that are not Own_Messages and whose Sequence_Number is greater than the Read_Marker.
- **Unread_Increase**: An event in which the Unread_Count becomes greater than its previous value. This happens either because a Chat_Message is counted under Requirement 2 criterion 5, or because a Chat_History sets a higher Unread_Count under Requirement 2 criteria 7 and 8. The Chat_History case is intended and includes the first Chat_History after the Board_Page loads and the first Chat_History after a reconnect, whenever either one raises the Unread_Count above its previous value (for example from 0 to the number of unread Chat_Messages it holds). A change that leaves the Unread_Count equal to or lower than its previous value is not an Unread_Increase.
- **New_Message_Attention_Animation**: The pulse ring animation played on the Chat_Launcher after an Unread_Increase (Requirement 24).
- **Unread_Ring**: The static two-tone ring drawn around the Chat_Launcher while the Unread_Count is greater than 0 and Panel_State is `closed` or `minimized`, made of an Inner_Band and an Outer_Band (Requirement 24 criterion 5).
- **Inner_Band**: The band of the Unread_Ring that lies directly against the edge of the Chat_Launcher, painted with `--text-on-primary` (`#ffffff`).
- **Outer_Band**: The band of the Unread_Ring that lies directly outside the Inner_Band and against the Board_Page content behind the ring, painted with `--text-primary` (`#1a1a2e`).
- **Unread_Dot**: The static dot shown in the minimized Panel_Header next to the Unread_Count while the Unread_Count is greater than 0 (Requirement 24).
- **At_Bottom**: The condition in which the Message_List is scrolled to within 48 CSS pixels of its end.
- **New_Messages_Indicator**: The control inside the Message_List that reports Chat_Messages received while the list is not At_Bottom and scrolls the list to its end when activated.
- **Presence_List**: The list in the Chat_Panel of the Participants currently in the Board_Session, taken from the participant list the Board_Page already receives.
- **Vote_Callout_Layer**: The existing fixed-position layer of the vote-reveal-fun-callouts feature on the Poker_Board_Page.
- **Moderator_Note_Balloon**: The existing fixed-position stack of moderator note balloons on the Poker_Board_Page (`.note-balloons` in `moderator-note-balloon.component.ts`).
- **Far_From_Average_Prompt**: The existing callout of the vote-reveal-fun-callouts feature (`far-from-average-prompt.component.ts`) shown inside the Vote_Callout_Layer, whose entrance uses the `callout-slide-in` keyframe.
- **Near_Average_Callout**: The existing near-average callout panel of the vote-reveal-fun-callouts feature (`NearAverageCalloutComponent`) shown inside the Vote_Callout_Layer.
- **Bottom_Left_Base_Offset**: The distance from the bottom edge of the viewport at which the bottom-left arrangement starts: 16 CSS pixels, except on a Narrow_Viewport while Panel_State is `minimized` or the Chat_Load_Error_Notice is shown, where it is the distance from the bottom edge of the viewport to a line 8 CSS pixels above the top edge of the minimized Panel_Header or of the Chat_Load_Error_Notice.
- **Upgrade_Log_Line**: The log line that `server.ts` writes for each WebSocket upgrade request.
- **WS_Max_Payload**: The `maxPayload` option of a `ws` `WebSocketServer`, the largest message in bytes that the server accepts; set to `WS_MAX_PAYLOAD_BYTES` for both the poker `wss` and `retroWss`.
- **Oversized_Frame**: A WebSocket message from a client whose payload is larger than `WS_MAX_PAYLOAD_BYTES` bytes (1,048,577 bytes or more), counting the combined payload of all fragments of one message.
- **Client_Frame_Guard**: The check in `WebSocketService` and `RetroWebSocketService` that measures the UTF-8 byte length of a serialised outgoing frame before sending it.
- **Current_Socket**: The WebSocket that `WebSocketService` or `RetroWebSocketService` created most recently through `connect()` or a scheduled reconnection attempt, until the service creates another one or is disconnected manually.
- **Stale_Socket**: Any WebSocket that `WebSocketService` or `RetroWebSocketService` created earlier and that is not its Current_Socket, including the socket closed by `disconnect()`.
- **Connection_Generation**: The read-only counter that `WebSocketService` and `RetroWebSocketService` each expose, starting at 0 when the service is created and increasing by exactly 1 on each `open` event of the Current_Socket (Requirement 25).
- **Connection_Episode_Reducer**: The existing pure `reduceEpisode` function in `client/src/app/services/connection-episode.ts`, shared by both client WebSocket services, that decides reconnection and notification effects from socket events.
- **Count of consecutive 1009 closes**: The count, kept in the state of the Connection_Episode_Reducer, of consecutive closes of a Board_Connection with close code 1009. A successful `open` leaves the count unchanged; the events of Requirement 23 criterion 13 reset the count to 0.
- **Bulk_Import_Panel**: The existing bulk-import area of `IssueListPanelComponent` on the Poker_Board_Page, shown while its `showBulkImport` signal is true, whose text area holds the Bulk_Import_Text.
- **Bulk_Import_Text**: The text held in the `bulkText` signal of `IssueListPanelComponent`, which `bulkImport()` splits into issue titles and sends in one `issue:add` frame.
- **Narrow_Viewport**: A viewport whose width is less than 768 CSS pixels, matching the existing `max-width: 767px` breakpoint.
- **Wide_Viewport**: A viewport whose width is 768 CSS pixels or more.
- **Version_Carrying_Files**: The three files whose version references change for a release: `helm/scrum-poker/Chart.yaml`, `helm/scrum-poker/README.md` and the root `README.md`.
- **Release_Commit**: The commit that contains the Chat_Feature together with the version changes of Requirement 26, and that the maintainer tags `v0.0.9`.

### Requirement 1: Chat launcher on both boards

#### Acceptance Criteria

WHILE the Poker_Board_Page shows its session-found branch, THE Chat_Feature SHALL render exactly one Chat_Launcher, bound to the Poker_Session whose ID is the `sessionId` route parameter of that page, so that the Chat_Launcher and its Chat_Panel send and receive chat events only over the poker Board_Connection of that page, whatever the Connection_State.
2. WHILE the Retro_Board_Page is shown, THE Chat_Feature SHALL render exactly one Chat_Launcher, bound to the Retro_Session whose ID is the `sessionId` route parameter of that page, so that the Chat_Launcher and its Chat_Panel send and receive chat events only over the retro Board_Connection of that page, whatever the Connection_State.
3. WHILE the Poker_Board_Page shows its "Session Not Found" branch, THE Chat_Feature SHALL render no Chat_Launcher and no Chat_Panel, including when the page switches to that branch while Panel_State is `open` or `minimized`.
4. THE Chat_Feature SHALL render the Chat_Launcher as a circle with `position: fixed`, 16 CSS pixels from the right edge and 16 CSS pixels from the bottom edge of the viewport, with a visible size of 56 by 56 CSS pixels on a Wide_Viewport and 48 by 48 CSS pixels on a Narrow_Viewport, except while Requirement 4 criterion 4 hides the Chat_Launcher.
5. THE Chat_Feature SHALL render the Chat_Launcher and the Chat_Panel outside every Interaction_Blocker wrapper, so that neither element becomes `inert` and the Chat_Launcher stays activatable while Connection_State is `reconnecting` or `disconnected`.
6. WHEN the user activates the Chat_Launcher by pointer click or tap, or by pressing Enter or Space while the Chat_Launcher has keyboard focus, while Panel_State is `closed` or `minimized`, THE Chat_Feature SHALL set Panel_State to `open`.
7. WHEN the user activates the Chat_Launcher by pointer click or tap, or by pressing Enter or Space while the Chat_Launcher has keyboard focus, while Panel_State is `open` on a Wide_Viewport, THE Chat_Feature SHALL set Panel_State to `closed`.
8. WHEN a Board_Page is rendered in a browser tab for a Board_Session, including after a page reload, THE Chat_Feature SHALL start with Panel_State `closed` and SHALL not restore an earlier Panel_State from any browser storage.
9. WHEN the Board_Page component is destroyed because the user navigates to another route, including the navigation to `/lobby` after `retro:session:ended` and the navigation after the client WebSocket service gives up reconnecting, THE Chat_Feature SHALL remove the Chat_Launcher and the Chat_Panel of that Board_Page from the document.
10. THE Chat_Feature SHALL hold Panel_State separately in each browser tab, so that changing Panel_State in one tab leaves the Panel_State of every other tab open on the same Board_Session unchanged.
11. WHILE the Board_Session of a Board_Page is an Active_Board_Session, THE Chat_Feature SHALL keep the Chat_Launcher, the Chat_Panel, the Message_List and the Composer available with the same behaviour, and SHALL decide whether a Draft is sendable only by Requirement 7 criterion 6, whatever the poker round state (no round, voting open, cards revealed), the voting state of any Participant, the countdown, the selected issue, the retro phase (cards hidden or revealed, voting enabled or disabled) and the retro `isCompleted` flag.
12. WHILE a Board_Session is an Active_Board_Session, THE Poker_Chat_Handler and the Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL accept or reject each chat send event only by the checks of Requirement 14 criterion 11, with no check of the poker round state, the voting state, the countdown, the retro phase or the retro `isCompleted` flag.

WHEN a Board_Session stops being an Active_Board_Session, THE System SHALL end the chat of that Board_Session as defined in Requirement 18 criteria 7 and 14, and no other board event (including `story:submit`, `cards:reveal`, `round:revote`, `issue:select`, `history:clear`, `board:clear`, `participant:remove`, `retro:cards:reveal`, `retro:voting:enable` and `retro:board:complete`) SHALL end, clear or disable the chat of that Board_Session for its remaining Participants. A Participant removed through `participant:remove` loses access to the chat only as defined in Requirement 15 criteria 12 and 13.

### Requirement 2: Unread badge

**User Story:** As a Participant, I want to see how many messages I have not read, so that I know when the team is talking while the chat is closed.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHILE the Unread_Count is between 1 and 99 inclusive, THE Chat_Launcher SHALL display the Unread_Badge with the Unread_Count as a decimal number, and SHALL show each new Unread_Count value within 100 ms of the change.
2. WHILE the Unread_Count is greater than 99, THE Chat_Launcher SHALL display the Unread_Badge with the text `99+`.
3. WHILE the Unread_Count is 0, THE Chat_Launcher SHALL hide the Unread_Badge.
4. WHILE Panel_State is `open`, `document.visibilityState` is `visible` and the Chat_Store holds at least one confirmed Chat_Message, THE Chat_Store SHALL set the Read_Marker to the greater of the current Read_Marker and the highest Sequence_Number among the confirmed Chat_Messages, within 100 ms of entering this state or of a Chat_Message or Chat_History being added in this state, so that the Unread_Count is 0 and the Read_Marker never decreases.
5. WHEN a Chat_Message that is not an Own_Message and whose `id` is not already in the Chat_Store is added to the Chat_Store while Panel_State is `closed` or `minimized`, or while `document.visibilityState` is `hidden`, THE Chat_Store SHALL increase the Unread_Count by exactly 1.
6. WHEN an Own_Message is added to the Chat_Store, including a `pending` Own_Message and an Own_Message sent from another tab of the same user, THE Chat_Store SHALL leave the Unread_Count unchanged.
7. WHEN a Chat_History is received while Panel_State is `closed` or `minimized`, or while `document.visibilityState` is `hidden`, and the tab holds a valid Read_Marker for that Board_Kind and Board_Session ID, THE Chat_Store SHALL set the Unread_Count to the number of Chat_Messages in that Chat_History that are not Own_Messages and whose Sequence_Number is greater than the stored Read_Marker, replacing any earlier Unread_Count.
8. WHEN a Chat_History is received while Panel_State is `closed` or `minimized`, or while `document.visibilityState` is `hidden`, and the tab holds no valid Read_Marker for that Board_Kind and Board_Session ID, THE Chat_Store SHALL set the Unread_Count to the number of Chat_Messages in that Chat_History that are not Own_Messages, replacing any earlier Unread_Count.
9. WHILE Panel_State is `minimized`, THE Panel_Header SHALL display the Unread_Count in the same form that criteria 1 to 3 define for the Unread_Badge: a decimal number from 1 to 99, the text `99+` above 99, and no count at 0.
10. WHEN the Read_Marker changes, THE Chat_Store SHALL write it to `sessionStorage` under a key built from the Board_Kind and the Board_Session ID, holding only the Sequence_Number as a decimal integer string, with no message text, author name or other chat content.
11. IF the value stored in `sessionStorage` under the Read_Marker key for the current Board_Kind and Board_Session ID is not a decimal string of a positive integer, THEN THE Chat_Store SHALL treat the tab as holding no Read_Marker, apply criterion 8, and overwrite the stored value at the next Read_Marker change.
12. IF reading from or writing to `sessionStorage` throws an exception, THEN THE Chat_Store SHALL keep the Read_Marker in memory for the life of the Board_Page, keep computing the Unread_Count as criteria 1 to 9 define, and show no error to the user.
13. WHILE the Unread_Count is greater than 0 and Panel_State is `closed` or `minimized`, THE Chat_Launcher SHALL show the Unread_Ring of Requirement 24 criterion 5 in addition to the Unread_Badge, and SHALL play the New_Message_Attention_Animation only when Requirement 24 criteria 1, 2, 10 and 12 start, restart or continue it, so that the animation runs for at most 3,600 ms after the latest Unread_Increase while the Unread_Ring stays shown.

### Requirement 3: Panel open, minimize and close behaviour

**User Story:** As a Participant, I want to open, minimize and close the chat, so that it is there when I need it and out of the way when I am voting or writing cards.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHILE Panel_State is `open`, THE Chat_Feature SHALL show the Panel_Header, the Message_List and the Composer.
2. WHILE Panel_State is `minimized`, THE Chat_Feature SHALL show only the Panel_Header and the Chat_Launcher. The Panel_Header SHALL have its right edge aligned with the Chat_Launcher's right edge, its bottom edge 8 CSS pixels above the Chat_Launcher's top edge, and a width of `min(360px, 100vw - 32px)`.
3. WHILE Panel_State is `closed`, THE Chat_Feature SHALL show only the Chat_Launcher and SHALL render no part of the Chat_Panel.
4. WHEN the user activates the minimize control of the Panel_Header with the pointer, Enter or Space while Panel_State is `open`, THE Chat_Feature SHALL set Panel_State to `minimized` whatever the Connection_State.
5. WHEN the user activates the restore control of the minimized Panel_Header with the pointer, Enter or Space, THE Chat_Feature SHALL set Panel_State to `open` whatever the Connection_State. The restore control SHALL cover the title and the Unread_Count area of the Panel_Header and SHALL exclude the close control.
6. WHEN the user activates the close control of the Panel_Header with the pointer, Enter or Space while Panel_State is `open` or `minimized`, THE Chat_Feature SHALL set Panel_State to `closed` whatever the Connection_State.
7. WHEN the user presses the Escape key while keyboard focus is inside the Chat_Panel, Panel_State is `open` or `minimized`, and no input-method composition is in progress (`KeyboardEvent.isComposing` is false), THE Chat_Feature SHALL set Panel_State to `closed` and keep the Draft unchanged.
8. WHEN Panel_State changes from `closed` or `minimized` to `open`, THE Chat_Feature SHALL restore the Draft and the Composer caret position that were present when the Chat_Panel was last `open` for the same Board_Session in the same tab. If the Message_List was At_Bottom at that moment, the Chat_Feature SHALL scroll the Message_List to its end. Otherwise it SHALL restore the Message_List's vertical scroll offset to within 1 CSS pixel of the stored value.
9. WHEN Panel_State changes to `open` for the first time on a Board_Page, THE Chat_Feature SHALL scroll the Message_List to its end. If no Chat_History has arrived yet, the Chat_Feature SHALL scroll to the end once the first Chat_History is rendered.
10. THE Chat_Feature SHALL hold the Draft in memory only and write it to no `localStorage`, `sessionStorage` or server event. The Draft SHALL be kept across Panel_State changes, Narrow_Viewport and Wide_Viewport layout switches, and Connection_State changes, and SHALL be discarded when the user leaves the Board_Page or the Board_Page connects to a different Board_Session.
11. WHILE Panel_State is `open` or `minimized` on a Wide_Viewport, THE Chat_Feature SHALL render no backdrop and no overlay outside the Chat_Panel's bounding box. Every Board_Page control whose bounding box lies outside the Chat_Panel's bounding box SHALL receive pointer clicks and SHALL stay reachable with Tab and Shift+Tab and activatable from the keyboard.
12. WHEN Panel_State changes from `open` to `minimized`, THE Chat_Feature SHALL move keyboard focus to the restore control of the minimized Panel_Header.
13. WHEN Panel_State changes between `open`, `minimized` and `closed`, THE Chat_Feature SHALL leave the Chat_Messages, the Delivery_States and the `pending` acknowledgement timers in the Chat_Store unchanged.

### Requirement 4: Responsive layout

**User Story:** As a Participant on a phone or a laptop, I want the chat to fit my screen, so that I can read and type without the board breaking.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHILE the viewport is a Wide_Viewport and Panel_State is `open`, THE Chat_Panel SHALL be 360 CSS pixels wide, at most `min(520px, 70vh)` tall, have its right edge 16 CSS pixels from the right edge of the viewport (aligned with the Chat_Launcher's right edge), and have its bottom edge 8 CSS pixels above the Chat_Launcher's top edge.
2. WHILE the viewport is a Wide_Viewport and Panel_State is `open`, THE Chat_Panel SHALL keep its top edge at least 8 CSS pixels below the bottom edge of the Board_Page header, reducing the Chat_Panel height below `min(520px, 70vh)` when needed. This constraint takes precedence over the height in criterion 1. While the height is reduced, the Panel_Header and the Composer SHALL stay fully visible, and the Message_List SHALL take up the remaining height and scroll vertically inside the Chat_Panel.
3. WHILE the viewport is a Narrow_Viewport and Panel_State is `open`, THE Chat_Panel SHALL have its top-left corner at the top-left corner of the viewport, its width equal to the viewport width and its height equal to `100dvh`. The Panel_Header SHALL sit at the top of the Chat_Panel, the Composer at the bottom, and the Message_List SHALL fill the space between them and scroll vertically.
4. WHILE the viewport is a Narrow_Viewport and Panel_State is `open`, THE Chat_Feature SHALL hide the Chat_Launcher and show the close control in the Panel_Header.
5. WHILE the viewport is a Narrow_Viewport, Panel_State is `open` and `window.visualViewport.height` is less than `window.innerHeight` (the on-screen keyboard is shown), THE Chat_Panel SHALL, within 100 ms of each `resize` or `scroll` event of `window.visualViewport`, place the bounding boxes of the Composer text area and the send button entirely inside the rectangle reported by `window.visualViewport`. The Message_List height SHALL shrink to make room for them.
6. WHEN the viewport width crosses the 768 CSS pixel breakpoint in either direction, in any Panel_State, THE Chat_Feature SHALL switch to the layout of the new viewport class (criteria 1–4 and 9) and SHALL keep the Panel_State, the Draft, the Message_List contents, the element that holds keyboard focus inside the Chat_Feature, and the At_Bottom condition of the Message_List if it held before the switch.
7. WHILE the viewport width is 320 CSS pixels, in each Panel_State (`closed`, `minimized` and `open`), THE Chat_Feature SHALL cause no horizontal scrolling: `scrollWidth` SHALL be at most `clientWidth` for both the document element and the Chat_Panel (WCAG 2.1 success criterion 1.4.10).
8. THE Message_List SHALL keep every message bubble no wider than the content box of the Message_List. Inside the bubble it SHALL break the `text` and the `authorName` of a Chat_Message onto further lines, including an unbroken run of up to `CHAT_MESSAGE_MAX_LENGTH` non-whitespace characters such as a long URL, so that no Chat_Message makes the Message_List scroll horizontally at any viewport width from 320 CSS pixels upward.
9. WHILE Panel_State is `minimized`, THE Panel_Header SHALL be docked with its bottom edge 8 CSS pixels above the Chat_Launcher's top edge and its right edge 16 CSS pixels from the right edge of the viewport. Its width SHALL be 360 CSS pixels on a Wide_Viewport and the viewport width minus 32 CSS pixels on a Narrow_Viewport.
10. WHILE the viewport is a Narrow_Viewport and Panel_State is `open`, THE Chat_Feature SHALL prevent the Board_Page behind the Chat_Panel from scrolling. WHEN Panel_State leaves `open` or the viewport becomes a Wide_Viewport, THE Chat_Feature SHALL restore the Board_Page scroll position it had before the Chat_Panel opened.
11. IF `window.visualViewport` is undefined in the browser, THEN THE Chat_Panel SHALL use the Narrow_Viewport layout of criterion 3 without the keyboard adjustment of criterion 5, and SHALL raise no script error.

### Requirement 5: Message list rendering

**User Story:** As a Participant, I want each message to show who sent it and when, with my own messages clearly marked, so that I can follow the conversation.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Message_List SHALL render confirmed Chat_Messages in ascending order of Sequence_Number, followed by every Unsequenced_Own_Message (Delivery_State `pending`, `failed`, or `sent` after a `CHAT_DUPLICATE` rejection under Requirement 8 criterion 3) in the order the user first sent them.
2. THE Message_List SHALL render, for each confirmed Chat_Message, the `authorName`, an avatar, the `text` and a timestamp derived from `sentAt`, and SHALL wrap an `authorName` of any length inside the message row without horizontal scrolling of the Message_List.
3. THE Message_List SHALL render the avatar as a circle holding the uppercased first Unicode code point of each of the first two whitespace-separated words of `authorName` (one initial when `authorName` has one word, and the character `?` when `authorName` has no non-whitespace character), with a background colour taken from a fixed palette of 8 colours by a mapping that gives the same `authorId` the same colour on every client and after every page reload.
4. THE Chat_Feature SHALL use only avatar palette colours that reach a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against the avatar's initials colour.
5. THE Message_List SHALL render the timestamp inside a `<time>` element whose `datetime` attribute holds the `sentAt` value, as 24-hour, zero-padded local time in `HH:mm` form (for example `09:05`) when `sentAt` falls on the same calendar day as the moment of rendering in the browser's local time zone, and otherwise as `MMM d, HH:mm` with an English three-letter month abbreviation and an unpadded day (for example `May 3, 09:05`).
6. THE Message_List SHALL render every Own_Message, including `pending` and `failed` Own_Messages, aligned to the inline end of the list with the label "You" in place of the avatar, and every other Chat_Message aligned to the inline start of the list.
7. THE Message_List SHALL render Own_Messages with a computed background colour that differs from the computed background colour of other Chat_Messages, with each of the two backgrounds reaching a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against its message text.
8. THE Message_List SHALL render the `text` and the `authorName` of every Chat_Message as plain text through Angular text interpolation, so that markup in either field is displayed literally and is never parsed as HTML.
9. THE Message_List SHALL display each `\n` in the `text` of a Chat_Message as the start of a new visual line, so that a `text` with k `\n` characters renders as at least k + 1 lines.
10. WHILE the Chat_Store has received at least one Chat_History since the Board_Connection most recently opened and holds no Chat_Message, including no Unsequenced_Own_Message, THE Message_List SHALL display the empty-state text "No messages yet. Say hello to your team."
11. WHILE the Chat_Store has not received a Chat_History since the Board_Connection first opened and holds no Chat_Message, THE Message_List SHALL display a visible loading text indicating that messages are being loaded, and SHALL NOT display the empty-state text.
12. WHEN the author of a Chat_Message leaves or is removed from the Board_Session, THE Message_List SHALL keep displaying that Chat_Message with the `authorName`, avatar and timestamp it showed before the author left.
13. IF no Chat_History arrives within `CHAT_ACK_TIMEOUT_MS` after Connection_State first becomes `connected` for the Board_Connection, THEN THE Message_List SHALL replace the loading text with a visible message indicating that earlier messages could not be loaded, keep the Composer usable, and render the Chat_History if it arrives later.
14. IF a chat added event or a Chat_History contains a value that `isChatMessage` rejects or whose `sentAt` does not parse as a valid date, THEN THE Chat_Store SHALL discard that value, render nothing for it in the Message_List, and keep every other Chat_Message of the same event unchanged.

### Requirement 6: Auto-scroll and new-messages indicator

**User Story:** As a Participant, I want the chat to follow new messages when I am at the bottom and to leave me alone when I am reading older ones, so that I never lose my place.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHILE Panel_State is `open`, WHEN a Chat_Message is added to the Message_List (including a Chat_Message from another tab, a Chat_Message received through the added event, or a Chat_Message added by a Chat_History after a reconnect) and the Message_List was At_Bottom immediately before the addition, THE Message_List SHALL scroll to its end so that it is At_Bottom within 300 ms after the added Chat_Message is rendered.
2. WHILE Panel_State is `open`, WHEN a Chat_Message not sent from this tab's Composer is added to the Message_List and the Message_List was not At_Bottom immediately before the addition, THE Message_List SHALL keep every Chat_Message that was visible in the Message_List viewport within 1 CSS pixel of its previous vertical position, including when the Chat_Store drops its oldest Chat_Message under Requirement 18 criterion 12. If the added Chat_Message is not an Own_Message, THE Message_List SHALL also show the New_Messages_Indicator with the count of non-Own_Message Chat_Messages added since the Message_List was last At_Bottom. If the added Chat_Message is an Own_Message from another tab of the same user, THE Message_List SHALL leave that count unchanged.
3. WHEN the user sends a Draft from the Composer, THE Message_List SHALL scroll to its end within 300 ms after the `pending` Own_Message is rendered, whatever its previous scroll position, and SHALL reset the New_Messages_Indicator count to 0 and hide the New_Messages_Indicator.
4. WHEN the user activates the New_Messages_Indicator with the pointer or the keyboard, THE Message_List SHALL scroll to its end within 300 ms, reset the New_Messages_Indicator count to 0, hide the New_Messages_Indicator and move keyboard focus to the Composer text area.
5. WHEN the Message_List becomes At_Bottom through user scrolling by any input method (mouse wheel, touch, keyboard or scrollbar drag), THE Message_List SHALL reset the New_Messages_Indicator count to 0 and hide the New_Messages_Indicator.
6. WHERE the operating system reports `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, THE Message_List SHALL perform every scroll to its end required by criteria 1, 3, 4 and 9 as a single jump with no intermediate scroll positions and no smooth-scroll animation.
7. WHILE the New_Messages_Indicator is shown, THE Message_List SHALL render it as a native `<button>` pinned to the bottom edge of the Message_List viewport. The button text SHALL be "1 new message" when the count is 1, "N new messages" when the count is between 2 and 99, and "99+ new messages" when the count is greater than 99.
8. WHILE Panel_State is `open`, WHEN a Chat_History arrives after a reconnect and the Message_List was not At_Bottom immediately before it arrived, THE Message_List SHALL keep every previously visible Chat_Message that is still in the Chat_History within 1 CSS pixel of its previous vertical position. THE Message_List SHALL also increase the New_Messages_Indicator count by the number of non-Own_Message Chat_Messages in that Chat_History whose Sequence_Number is greater than the highest Sequence_Number the Chat_Store held before the Chat_History arrived.
9. WHEN Panel_State changes from `closed` or `minimized` to `open`, and the Chat_Panel has been `open` at least once before on the same Board_Page, THE Message_List SHALL do one of two things. If the Message_List was At_Bottom when Panel_State last left `open`, it SHALL scroll to its end. Otherwise it SHALL restore the scroll position under Requirement 3 criterion 8 and show the New_Messages_Indicator counting every non-Own_Message Chat_Message added since the Message_List was last At_Bottom, including those added while Panel_State was not `open`.

### Requirement 7: Composer

**User Story:** As a Participant, I want a comfortable text box that sends on Enter and lets me write multi-line messages, so that typing in the chat feels like any other chat tool.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Composer SHALL provide a multi-line text area that is 1 visible line tall when the Draft is empty, grows by one visible line per rendered line of the Draft (counting both line breaks and soft-wrapped lines) up to 5 visible lines, shrinks again as rendered lines are removed, and scrolls internally while the Draft renders more than 5 lines.
2. WHEN the user presses Enter without Shift in the Composer and the Draft is sendable, THE Composer SHALL send the Draft, set the Draft to the empty string and return the text area to 1 visible line, without inserting a line break.
3. WHEN the user presses Shift+Enter in the Composer, THE Composer SHALL insert one line break into the Draft at the caret position and send nothing.
4. WHILE an input-method composition is in progress in the Composer (`KeyboardEvent.isComposing` is true), THE Composer SHALL pass Enter to the input method, leave the Draft to the input method and send nothing.
5. WHEN the user activates the send button and the Draft is sendable, THE Composer SHALL send the Draft, set the Draft to the empty string and return the text area to 1 visible line.
6. THE Composer SHALL treat the Draft as sendable exactly when the Chat_Validator returns a Normalised_Text (not a `ChatRejection` code) for the Draft and Connection_State is `connected`; no other condition SHALL make the Draft sendable or unsendable.
7. WHILE the Draft is not sendable, THE Composer SHALL render the send button with the `disabled` attribute, and SHALL treat Enter without Shift as a no-op that sends nothing, inserts no line break and leaves the Draft unchanged.
8. THE Composer SHALL keep the Draft at no more than `CHAT_MESSAGE_MAX_LENGTH` (500) UTF-16 code units, measured on the Draft as typed before normalisation, and SHALL ignore typed input that would exceed that length.
9. WHILE the Draft holds 400 or more UTF-16 code units, THE Composer SHALL display a counter in the form `<length>/500`, where `<length>` is the current Draft length in UTF-16 code units; WHILE the Draft holds fewer than 400 UTF-16 code units, THE Composer SHALL hide the counter.
10. WHEN the Draft is sent by Enter or by the send button, THE Composer SHALL leave keyboard focus in the text area, or move it there if the send button held focus.
11. WHEN the Composer sends a Draft, THE Composer SHALL send the Normalised_Text of the Draft (not the raw Draft), a Client_Message_Id newly generated for that send in canonical 36-character UUID form and different from every Client_Message_Id previously generated in the tab, and the Board_Session ID of the Board_Page.
12. WHEN the user pastes text that would make the Draft longer than `CHAT_MESSAGE_MAX_LENGTH` UTF-16 code units, THE Composer SHALL insert only the leading part of the pasted text that brings the Draft to exactly `CHAT_MESSAGE_MAX_LENGTH` UTF-16 code units, or 1 code unit fewer when the cut would split a surrogate pair, and SHALL discard the rest of the pasted text.

### Requirement 8: Delivery states, retry and acknowledgement

**User Story:** As a Participant, I want to see whether my message was delivered and to retry it if it failed, so that I am never unsure whether my team saw it.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Composer sends a Draft, THE Chat_Store SHALL add an Own_Message with Delivery_State `pending`, the Normalised_Text of the Draft and the Client_Message_Id of the send attempt to the Message_List, after every confirmed Chat_Message and after every earlier Unsequenced_Own_Message, showing the text "Sending…" in place of the timestamp.
2. WHEN a Chat_Message arrives whose `authorId` is the current user and whose `clientMessageId` matches a `pending` or `failed` Own_Message, THE Chat_Store SHALL replace that Own_Message with the arrived Chat_Message, set its Delivery_State to `sent`, stop its `CHAT_ACK_TIMEOUT_MS` timer, remove any rejection message shown next to it, and place it among the confirmed Chat_Messages by its Sequence_Number, so that the Chat_Store holds exactly one entry for that Client_Message_Id.
3. WHEN a rejection with code `CHAT_DUPLICATE` arrives for the Client_Message_Id of a `pending` or `failed` Own_Message, THE Chat_Store SHALL set that Own_Message's Delivery_State to `sent`, stop its `CHAT_ACK_TIMEOUT_MS` timer, remove any rejection message and Retry and Discard controls shown for it, replace the "Sending…" text with a timestamp derived from the local time of the first send attempt, and keep it as an Unsequenced_Own_Message in its send-order position under Requirement 5 criterion 1.
4. WHEN a rejection with any code other than `CHAT_DUPLICATE` arrives for the Client_Message_Id of a `pending` or `failed` Own_Message, THE Chat_Store SHALL set that Own_Message's Delivery_State to `failed`, stop its `CHAT_ACK_TIMEOUT_MS` timer, keep its text and Client_Message_Id unchanged, and show the `message` field of the rejection next to it, replacing any rejection message shown earlier for that Own_Message.
5. IF a `pending` Own_Message has received neither a Chat_Message with its Client_Message_Id nor a rejection for its Client_Message_Id after `CHAT_ACK_TIMEOUT_MS` (10,000 ms) of accumulated time in which Connection_State was `connected`, counted from its most recent send or resend, THEN THE Chat_Store SHALL set that Own_Message's Delivery_State to `failed` and show a message next to it indicating that delivery could not be confirmed.
6. WHILE an Own_Message has Delivery_State `failed`, THE Message_List SHALL show a Retry control and a Discard control for that Own_Message, and SHALL show neither control for an Own_Message with Delivery_State `pending` or `sent`.
7. WHEN the user activates Retry on a `failed` Own_Message while Connection_State is `connected`, THE Chat_Store SHALL resend that Own_Message with its original Client_Message_Id and original text, set its Delivery_State to `pending`, remove the rejection message shown next to it, and restart its `CHAT_ACK_TIMEOUT_MS` timer from 0 ms.
8. WHEN the user activates Discard on a `failed` Own_Message, THE Chat_Store SHALL remove that Own_Message from the Chat_Store and the Message_List and stop its `CHAT_ACK_TIMEOUT_MS` timer, and SHALL add any Chat_Message with that Client_Message_Id that arrives later as a confirmed Chat_Message ordered by Sequence_Number.
9. WHEN a rejection with code `CHAT_RATE_LIMITED` arrives, THE Composer SHALL show the text "You're sending messages too fast. Try again in N s.", where N is the remaining time of `retryAfterMs` measured from receipt of the rejection, rounded up to whole seconds, updated at least once per second, replaced by any later `CHAT_RATE_LIMITED` rejection, and removed when `retryAfterMs` has elapsed since receipt.
10. THE Message_List SHALL render the Delivery_State of each `pending` Own_Message as the visible text "Sending…" and of each `failed` Own_Message as the visible text "Not sent", so that the state is not conveyed by colour alone.
11. WHILE an Own_Message has Delivery_State `failed` and Connection_State is not `connected`, THE Message_List SHALL render the Retry control of that Own_Message as disabled and the Discard control as enabled.
12. IF a rejection arrives whose `clientMessageId` matches no `pending` or `failed` Own_Message in the Chat_Store, THEN THE Chat_Store SHALL ignore the rejection, leaving every Own_Message and its Delivery_State unchanged, except that a `CHAT_RATE_LIMITED` rejection SHALL still show the Composer notice of criterion 9.

### Requirement 9: Behaviour during disconnect and reconnect

**User Story:** As a Participant on an unstable network, I want the chat to keep my messages and catch up after a reconnect, so that I neither lose nor duplicate messages.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHILE Connection_State is not `connected`, THE Chat_Panel SHALL keep rendering every Chat_Message and Own_Message held in the Chat_Store, keep the Message_List scrollable by pointer, wheel and keyboard, keep the Panel_State unchanged, and show the status text "Chat is offline until the connection is restored." above the Composer.
2. WHILE Connection_State is not `connected`, THE Composer SHALL keep the text area editable, keep the Draft unchanged, insert a line break on Shift+Enter, disable the send button and send nothing on Enter.
3. WHILE Connection_State is not `connected`, THE Chat_Store SHALL suspend the `CHAT_ACK_TIMEOUT_MS` timer of every `pending` Own_Message and keep each such Own_Message in Delivery_State `pending` with the text "Sending…", so that a disconnect of any duration sets no Own_Message to `failed`.
4. WHEN the first Chat_History after Connection_State changes from `reconnecting` or `disconnected` to `connected` arrives, THE Chat_Store SHALL replace all of its confirmed Chat_Messages with the Chat_Messages of that Chat_History in ascending Sequence_Number order, and keep every remaining Unsequenced_Own_Message after the confirmed Chat_Messages in the order the Own_Messages were first sent. Criteria 4 to 7 apply only to the Chat_History that Requirement 25 criterion 11 identifies as that first Chat_History by its Connection_Generation; a Chat_History ignored under Requirement 25 criterion 12 triggers none of them.
5. WHEN the first Chat_History after Connection_State changes from `reconnecting` or `disconnected` to `connected` arrives, THE Chat_Store SHALL replace every `pending` or `failed` Own_Message whose Client_Message_Id appears in that Chat_History with the matching Chat_Message of that Chat_History, with Delivery_State `sent`, so that the Message_List shows exactly one entry per Client_Message_Id.
6. WHEN the first Chat_History after Connection_State changes from `reconnecting` or `disconnected` to `connected` arrives, THE Chat_Store SHALL resend every `pending` Own_Message whose Client_Message_Id does not appear in that Chat_History exactly once, in the order the Own_Messages were first sent, with the original Client_Message_Id and the original Normalised_Text, and start a new full `CHAT_ACK_TIMEOUT_MS` timer for each resent Own_Message, after which Requirement 8 criteria 2 to 5 decide its Delivery_State.
7. WHEN the first Chat_History after Connection_State changes from `reconnecting` or `disconnected` to `connected` arrives, THE Chat_Store SHALL keep every `failed` Own_Message whose Client_Message_Id does not appear in that Chat_History in Delivery_State `failed`, with its Retry and Discard controls, and SHALL not resend it automatically.
8. IF the client WebSocket service gives up reconnecting and navigates away, THEN THE Chat_Feature SHALL discard the Chat_Store contents, including every `pending` and `failed` Own_Message and the Draft, together with the Board_Page.
9. WHILE Connection_State is not `connected`, THE Message_List SHALL disable the Retry control of every `failed` Own_Message and keep its Discard control operable.
10. WHEN Connection_State changes from `reconnecting` or `disconnected` to `connected`, THE Chat_Panel SHALL remove the offline status text, and THE Composer SHALL enable sending if the Draft is sendable under Requirement 7 criterion 6, with the Draft content unchanged.

### Requirement 14: Server-side validation and normalisation

**User Story:** As an operator, I want every chat message checked at the server, so that malformed, oversized or deceptive text never reaches other users.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Chat_Validator SHALL be a pure function that takes one untrusted value and returns either the Normalised_Text or exactly one of the codes `CHAT_INVALID`, `CHAT_TOO_LONG` or `CHAT_EMPTY`, with no input mutation, no network access, no clock reads, and the same result for the same input on every call.
2. IF the text value is not a string (including `undefined`, `null`, a number, a boolean, an array or an object), THEN THE Chat_Validator SHALL return `CHAT_INVALID` and perform no further check.
3. IF the text value is a string longer than `CHAT_RAW_TEXT_MAX_LENGTH` (2,000) UTF-16 code units before normalisation, THEN THE Chat_Validator SHALL return `CHAT_TOO_LONG` without normalising the text, while a string of 2,000 or fewer UTF-16 code units proceeds to normalisation.
4. WHEN the Chat_Validator receives a string of at most `CHAT_RAW_TEXT_MAX_LENGTH` UTF-16 code units, THE Chat_Validator SHALL produce the Normalised_Text by, in this order, converting each `\r\n` pair and each remaining lone `\r` to `\n`, removing every Disallowed_Character, and removing leading and trailing whitespace, where whitespace means U+0009, U+000A, U+FEFF, U+2028, U+2029 and every character of Unicode category Zs, and SHALL leave every interior character unchanged, including interior tabs, interior spaces and consecutive line feeds.
5. IF the Normalised_Text is empty (0 UTF-16 code units), THEN THE Chat_Validator SHALL return `CHAT_EMPTY`.
6. IF the Normalised_Text is longer than `CHAT_MESSAGE_MAX_LENGTH` (500) UTF-16 code units, THEN THE Chat_Validator SHALL return `CHAT_TOO_LONG`, while a Normalised_Text of 1 to 500 UTF-16 code units is returned as accepted.
7. FOR ALL strings, applying the Chat_Validator normalisation to an already Normalised_Text SHALL return the same Normalised_Text (idempotence property).
8. FOR ALL strings accepted by the Chat_Validator, the Normalised_Text SHALL contain no Disallowed_Character and no `\r`, SHALL have no leading or trailing whitespace as defined in criterion 4, and SHALL be between 1 and `CHAT_MESSAGE_MAX_LENGTH` UTF-16 code units long (invariant property).
9. IF the `clientMessageId` of a send payload is not a string of exactly 36 characters made of five groups of 8, 4, 4, 4 and 12 hexadecimal digits (`0`–`9`, `a`–`f`, `A`–`F`) separated by hyphens, of any UUID version, THEN THE Poker_Chat_Handler and the Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL reject the send with `CHAT_INVALID`.
10. IF the payload of a chat send event is not a non-array object (it is `null`, a primitive or an array), THEN THE Poker_Chat_Handler and the Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL reject the send with `CHAT_INVALID` before the session match check.
11. THE Poker_Chat_Handler and the Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL apply their checks in the order (1) payload is a non-array object, (2) session match, which fails with `CHAT_SESSION_MISMATCH` when `sessionId` is not a string equal to the Board_Session ID of the sending Board_Connection, (3) `clientMessageId` form, (4) Chat_Validator on `text`, (5) duplicate check of Requirement 16, (6) rate limit of Requirement 17, and SHALL answer each send event with the outcome of the first check that does not pass only, so that a rejected send event produces exactly one rejected event.
12. IF any check fails, THEN THE Poker_Chat_Handler and the Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL store nothing, broadcast nothing, leave the Sequence_Number counter, the Dedup_Record and the Chat_Rate_Limiter state unchanged, send the rejection only to the Board_Connection that sent the event, and keep that Board_Connection open.
13. THE Poker_Chat_Handler and the Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL send rejections on the dedicated rejected event of their Board_Kind and SHALL leave the existing `error` and `retro:error` events unused for chat validation.
14. FOR ALL values, the Chat_Validator that the Chat_Feature runs on the client to decide whether a Draft is sendable SHALL return the same Normalised_Text or the same rejection code as the Chat_Validator run by the Poker_Chat_Handler and the Retro_Chat_Handler (client-server parity property).
15. WHEN the Poker_Chat_Handler or the Retro_Chat_Handler rejects a chat send event, THE Poker_Chat_Handler or the Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL send a rejection whose `clientMessageId` equals the payload's `clientMessageId` when that value is a string and the empty string otherwise, whose `code` is the `ChatRejection` code of the failing check, and whose `message` is a fixed, non-empty English text that states the reason for that code and contains none of the submitted text.

### Requirement 15: Authorization and session scoping

**User Story:** As a Participant, I want only the people in my session to read and write my session's chat, so that our conversation cannot leak to or be spoofed by anyone else.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a chat send event is accepted, THE Poker_Chat_Handler and the Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL set `authorId` to the user ID and `authorName` to the display name of the Authenticated_Identity of the sending Board_Connection, as held at the moment of acceptance, and SHALL ignore any `authorId`, `authorName` or other author field present in the payload.
2. IF a chat send payload is a non-array object whose `sessionId` is missing, is not a string, or differs from the Board_Session ID of the sending Board_Connection, THEN THE Poker_Chat_Handler and the Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL reject the send with `CHAT_SESSION_MISMATCH`, run none of the later checks of Requirement 14 criterion 11, store nothing and broadcast nothing, and the rejection SHALL carry the payload's `clientMessageId` unchanged if it is a string and an empty string otherwise.
3. IF a chat send event arrives on a poker Board_Connection whose Poker_Session is no longer in the `SessionRegistry`, THEN THE Poker_Chat_Handler SHALL answer that Board_Connection only with the existing `error` event carrying code `SESSION_NOT_FOUND`, keep the Board_Connection open, store nothing and broadcast nothing.
4. IF a chat send event arrives on a retro Board_Connection whose Retro_Session is no longer in the `RetroSessionRegistry`, THEN THE Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL follow the existing `NOT_FOUND` path, which sends the `retro:error` event with code `NOT_FOUND` to that Board_Connection and closes it with close code 4004, and SHALL store nothing and broadcast nothing.
5. WHEN a chat send event is accepted, THE Poker_Chat_Handler and the Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL send the resulting Chat_Message exactly once to every Board_Connection that is registered in the client map for that Board_Session and whose socket is open at broadcast time, including the sending Board_Connection and the author's other Board_Connections, and SHALL send it to no Board_Connection of any other Board_Session of either Board_Kind.
6. THE Poker_Chat_Handler SHALL store and broadcast poker Chat_Messages only through the `GameSession` and the poker client map, and THE Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL store and broadcast retro Chat_Messages only through the `RetroSession` and the retro client map.
7. IF a retro chat event name arrives on a poker Board_Connection, or a poker chat event name arrives on a retro Board_Connection, THEN the receiving handler SHALL answer through its existing unknown-event path with code `UNKNOWN_EVENT`, keep the Board_Connection open, and change no Chat_Buffer, Dedup_Record or Chat_Rate_Limiter state of either Board_Kind.
8. THE Poker_Chat_Handler and the Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL accept chat send events from every Participant with the role `moderator` or `participant`, including anonymous users, under every `revealPermission` and `issuePermission` mode (`moderator-only`, `all-players`, `select-specific`).
9. WHEN a Participant is removed from a Poker_Session by the moderator (`participant:remove`), THE Poker_Chat_Handler SHALL keep that Participant's earlier Chat_Messages in the Chat_Buffer with their original `authorId` and `authorName`, and SHALL include them in every later `chat:history` for that Poker_Session.
10. FOR ALL interleavings of chat send events across two or more Board_Sessions of either Board_Kind, each Board_Session's Chat_Buffer SHALL contain only Chat_Messages sent on Board_Connections of that Board_Session, and each Board_Connection SHALL receive only Chat_Messages and Chat_Histories of its own Board_Session (isolation property).
11. IF the client receives a chat event on a Board_Connection other than the one currently open for the Board_Kind and Board_Session ID shown in that tab, including a connection to a previously shown Board_Session that has not finished closing, THEN THE Chat_Store SHALL discard the event and leave its Chat_Messages, Delivery_States, Unread_Count and Read_Marker unchanged. Events of a Stale_Socket never reach the Chat_Store under Requirement 25 criterion 4, and the Chat_Store tells connections apart by Connection_Generation under Requirement 25 criteria 9, 11 and 12.
12. IF a chat send event arrives on a Board_Connection that is no longer registered in the client map of its Board_Session, including a connection of a Participant removed by the moderator whose close (code 4010) has not completed, THEN THE Poker_Chat_Handler and the Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL store nothing, broadcast nothing, and leave the Sequence_Number counter, the Dedup_Record and the Chat_Rate_Limiter state unchanged.
13. WHEN a Participant is removed from a Poker_Session by the moderator, THE Poker_Chat_Handler SHALL send no Chat_Message accepted after the removal to any Board_Connection of the removed user in that Poker_Session.

### Requirement 16: Identifiers, ordering and duplicate prevention

**User Story:** As a Participant, I want every participant to see messages in the same order and each message exactly once, so that the conversation reads the same for everyone.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a chat send event is accepted, THE Poker_Chat_Handler and the Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL assign the Chat_Message an `id` that is a UUID in canonical 36-character form and that no other Chat_Message of that Board_Session has. They SHALL also assign the next Sequence_Number of that Board_Session, and a `sentAt` value in ISO 8601 UTC form with millisecond precision. The `sentAt` value SHALL equal the server clock reading or the `sentAt` of the previous accepted Chat_Message of that Board_Session, whichever is later.
2. FOR ALL sequences of accepted chat send events in one Board_Session, the Sequence_Numbers SHALL start at 1 and increase by exactly 1 per accepted Chat_Message, with no Sequence_Number reused after older Chat_Messages are evicted from the Chat_Buffer. The `sentAt` values SHALL be non-decreasing in Sequence_Number order, including when the server clock moves backwards between two accepted Chat_Messages (invariant property).
3. IF a chat send event is rejected with any `ChatRejection` code, or is answered with a stored Chat_Message under criterion 5, THEN THE Poker_Chat_Handler and the Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL leave the Sequence_Number counter, the Chat_Buffer and the Dedup_Record of that Board_Session unchanged.
4. WHEN a chat send event is accepted, THE Poker_Chat_Handler and the Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL record its Client_Message_Id and Sequence_Number in the Dedup_Record under the author's user ID from the Authenticated_Identity. When an author already holds `CHAT_DEDUP_PER_AUTHOR` entries, the handlers SHALL remove that author's oldest entry before adding the new one, leaving the entries of other authors unchanged.
5. IF a chat send event carries a Client_Message_Id that the Dedup_Record holds for the same author user ID, and the Chat_Message with the recorded Sequence_Number is still in the Chat_Buffer, THEN THE Poker_Chat_Handler and the Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL send that stored Chat_Message, unchanged and regardless of the `text` in the new payload, on the added event of their Board_Kind. The handlers SHALL send it to the sending Board_Connection only and broadcast nothing to other Board_Connections.
6. IF a chat send event carries a Client_Message_Id that the Dedup_Record holds for the same author user ID, and the Chat_Message with the recorded Sequence_Number is no longer in the Chat_Buffer, THEN THE Poker_Chat_Handler and the Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL reject the send with `CHAT_DUPLICATE` and send the rejection to the sending Board_Connection only.
7. FOR ALL sequences of chat send events in which an author sends the same Client_Message_Id one or more times, with fewer than `CHAT_DEDUP_PER_AUTHOR` other Chat_Messages from that author accepted between the first acceptance and the last send, exactly one Chat_Message with that Client_Message_Id and author SHALL be added to the Chat_Buffer (idempotence property).
8. WHEN a Chat_Message arrives whose `id` is already in the Chat_Store, THE Chat_Store SHALL keep exactly one Chat_Message with that `id`. The number of Chat_Messages in the Message_List and the Unread_Count SHALL stay unchanged.
9. FOR ALL orders of arrival of the same set of confirmed Chat_Messages, including arrivals split between added events and a Chat_History, merging them into the Chat_Store SHALL produce the same list of confirmed Chat_Messages in ascending Sequence_Number order (confluence property).
10. FOR ALL Chat_Histories, applying the same Chat_History to the Chat_Store twice SHALL leave the confirmed Chat_Messages, the Delivery_State of every Own_Message and the Unread_Count the same as applying it once (idempotence property).
11. IF a chat send event carries a Client_Message_Id that the Dedup_Record holds only for a different author user ID, THEN THE Poker_Chat_Handler and the Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL treat the send as a new message, subject to the remaining checks of Requirement 14 criterion 11, and leave the other author's Dedup_Record entries and Chat_Messages unchanged.
12. THE Chat_Store SHALL hold at most one entry, counting `pending`, `failed` and `sent` Own_Messages together, for each Client_Message_Id of the current user.

### Requirement 17: Rate limiting

**User Story:** As a Participant, I want the chat protected from floods, so that one person or script cannot drown out the conversation or slow down the board.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a chat send event is accepted, THE Chat_Rate_Limiter SHALL record the acceptance time against the author's user ID from the Authenticated_Identity within that Board_Session, so that sends from every Board_Connection of that author in that Board_Session count toward one shared limit. This includes other tabs and connections opened after a reconnect.
2. IF the author already has `CHAT_RATE_LIMIT_COUNT` (5) accepted Chat_Messages in the Board_Session whose acceptance time t satisfies `now − t < CHAT_RATE_LIMIT_WINDOW_MS` (5,000 ms), THEN THE Poker_Chat_Handler and the Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL reject the send with `CHAT_RATE_LIMITED` and SHALL keep the sending Board_Connection open. The rejection SHALL carry a `retryAfterMs` equal to `(t_oldest + CHAT_RATE_LIMIT_WINDOW_MS) − now`, where t_oldest is the earliest of those acceptance times. The value SHALL be a whole number of milliseconds between 1 and `CHAT_RATE_LIMIT_WINDOW_MS` inclusive.
3. IF a chat send event is rejected with any `ChatRejection` code, including `CHAT_RATE_LIMITED`, THEN THE Chat_Rate_Limiter SHALL record no acceptance time for it, so that rejected sends neither count against the limit nor extend the window.
4. THE Poker_Chat_Handler and the Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL run the rate-limit check only after the duplicate check of Requirement 16 criteria 5 and 6. A send whose Client_Message_Id the Dedup_Record already holds for the same author SHALL be answered under Requirement 16 without being checked or counted by the Chat_Rate_Limiter, including while that author is at the limit.
5. THE System SHALL hold the Chat_Rate_Limiter state only inside the `GameSession` or `RetroSession` it belongs to. Deleting the Board_Session from its registry SHALL remove that state, and closing or reopening a Board_Connection SHALL leave the author's recorded acceptance times unchanged.
6. FOR ALL sequences of chat send events with timestamps, the number of Chat_Messages the Chat_Rate_Limiter accepts from one author in one Board_Session within any half-open interval `[t, t + CHAT_RATE_LIMIT_WINDOW_MS)` SHALL be at most `CHAT_RATE_LIMIT_COUNT` (invariant property).
7. THE Chat_Rate_Limiter SHALL take the current time, in milliseconds, from an injectable clock, and SHALL use that same clock both to record acceptance times and to evaluate the window, so that tests can control time.
8. WHEN an author sends a chat send event after the `retryAfterMs` of that author's most recent `CHAT_RATE_LIMITED` rejection in the Board_Session has elapsed, and the event passes the payload shape, session match, `clientMessageId` form, Chat_Validator and duplicate checks of Requirement 14 criterion 11, THE Poker_Chat_Handler and the Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL accept the send.
9. THE Chat_Rate_Limiter SHALL apply the same `CHAT_RATE_LIMIT_COUNT` and `CHAT_RATE_LIMIT_WINDOW_MS` to every author regardless of role, including the `moderator` and the session owner.
10. FOR ALL interleavings of chat send events by two or more authors across two or more Board_Sessions of either Board_Kind, whether the Chat_Rate_Limiter rejects one author's send in one Board_Session SHALL depend only on that author's accepted Chat_Messages in that same Board_Session (isolation property).

### Requirement 18: History buffer and lifecycle

**User Story:** As a Participant who joins late or reconnects, I want to see the recent conversation, and as an operator I want chat to disappear with the session, so that context is kept without unbounded memory use.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Chat_Buffer SHALL hold at most `CHAT_HISTORY_LIMIT` (200) Chat_Messages per Board_Session.
2. WHEN a Chat_Message is accepted while the Chat_Buffer holds `CHAT_HISTORY_LIMIT` Chat_Messages, THE Chat_Buffer SHALL remove the Chat_Message with the lowest Sequence_Number before adding the new one, leaving the Sequence_Number counter and the Dedup_Record unchanged by the removal and sending no event to any Board_Connection about the removed Chat_Message.
3. FOR ALL sequences of chat send events, the Chat_Buffer SHALL contain exactly the most recent `min(n, CHAT_HISTORY_LIMIT)` accepted Chat_Messages in ascending Sequence_Number order, where n is the number of send events that produced a new Chat_Message, excluding rejected send events and duplicate resends answered under Requirement 16 criterion 5 (model-based property).
4. WHEN a poker Board_Connection opens, including a reopened connection after a reconnect, THE Poker_Chat_Handler SHALL send `chat:history` to that Board_Connection only, after the existing `session:state` message and before any `chat:message:added` event to that Board_Connection, holding every Chat_Message of the Chat_Buffer in ascending Sequence_Number order, or an empty array when the Chat_Buffer is empty.
5. WHEN a retro Board_Connection opens, including a reopened connection after a reconnect, THE Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL send `retro:chat:history` to that Board_Connection only, after the existing `retro:session:state` message and before any `retro:chat:message:added` event to that Board_Connection, holding every Chat_Message of the Chat_Buffer in ascending Sequence_Number order, or an empty array when the Chat_Buffer is empty.
6. WHEN a new Board_Session is created, THE System SHALL give the Board_Session an empty Chat_Buffer, a Sequence_Number counter that issues 1 next, an empty Dedup_Record and an empty Chat_Rate_Limiter state.
7. THE System SHALL hold the Chat_Buffer, the Sequence_Number counter, the Dedup_Record and the Chat_Rate_Limiter state only inside the `GameSession` or `RetroSession` object, with no module-level mutable chat state, so that deleting the Board_Session from its registry through the existing delete route or the existing inactivity cleanup removes all of its chat state.
8. WHEN a poker moderator clears the voting history (`history:clear`) or clears the board (`board:clear`), THE Poker_Chat_Handler SHALL leave the Chat_Buffer, the Sequence_Number counter, the Dedup_Record and the Chat_Rate_Limiter state unchanged and send no chat event.
9. WHEN a retro moderator completes the board (`retro:board:complete`), THE Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL leave the Chat_Buffer, the Sequence_Number counter, the Dedup_Record and the Chat_Rate_Limiter state unchanged and keep accepting chat send events under the same checks as before completion.
10. WHEN a chat send event is accepted, THE System SHALL set the Board_Session's `lastActivityAt` to the server clock time of acceptance in ISO 8601 form, and IF a chat send event is rejected or answered as a duplicate under Requirement 16 criterion 5, THEN THE System SHALL leave `lastActivityAt` unchanged.
11. WHEN the user leaves a Board_Page, or the `:sessionId` route parameter of the Board_Page changes to a different Board_Session ID, THE Chat_Store SHALL discard all Chat_Messages, Delivery_States, pending `CHAT_ACK_TIMEOUT_MS` timers, the Draft and the New_Messages_Indicator count of the previous Board_Session, send no retry of the previous Board_Session's `pending` or `failed` Own_Messages, and leave the previous Board_Session's Read_Marker in `sessionStorage` unchanged.
12. WHEN a Chat_Message with a Sequence_Number is added to the Chat_Store while the Chat_Store holds `CHAT_HISTORY_LIMIT` Chat_Messages with a Sequence_Number, THE Chat_Store SHALL drop the Chat_Message with the lowest Sequence_Number, and SHALL neither count nor drop Unsequenced_Own_Messages under this limit.
13. WHEN the first Chat_History arrives on a Board_Connection after the Board_Page is rendered or after criterion 11 has discarded the previous Board_Session's state, THE Chat_Store SHALL set its Chat_Messages to the Chat_Messages of that Chat_History in ascending Sequence_Number order and end the loading state of Requirement 5 criterion 11, recording its Connection_Generation under Requirement 25 criterion 9 so that Requirement 25 criteria 11 and 12 decide every later Chat_History.
14. IF a Board_Connection opens for a Board_Session ID after that Board_Session has been deleted from its registry, THEN THE Poker_Chat_Handler and the Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL send no Chat_History and no Chat_Message from the deleted Board_Session to that Board_Connection.

### Requirement 19: Logging and data privacy

**User Story:** As an operator, I want chat to leave no message content or secrets in logs or browser storage, so that conversations stay private to the session.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Poker_Chat_Handler and the Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL write none of the following to any server log, for both accepted and rejected chat send events: the raw `text` of a chat send payload (the sent Draft), the Normalised_Text, the `text` or `authorName` of any Chat_Message, the raw payload object of any chat event, and the JWT of any Board_Connection.
2. WHEN the Poker_Chat_Handler or the Retro_Chat_Handler writes a server log entry about a chat event, THE Poker_Chat_Handler and the Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL limit that log entry to these fields: the Board_Kind, the Board_Session ID, the event name, the `ChatRejection` code (rejected sends only), the number of Chat_Messages in the Chat_Buffer, the number of Board_Connections the Chat_Message was broadcast to, and the length of the received `text` in UTF-16 code units.
3. THE Chat_Feature SHALL write no chat message text and no Draft text to the browser console, `localStorage`, `sessionStorage`, IndexedDB, cookies or the Cache Storage API. This applies equally when a chat event fails to parse, is rejected or times out.
4. THE Chat_Feature SHALL render chat content in every chat component, service and template without `innerHTML`, `outerHTML`, `insertAdjacentHTML`, `document.write`, Angular `[innerHTML]` or `[outerHTML]` template bindings, `Renderer2.setProperty` on an HTML-content property, or `DomSanitizer.bypassSecurityTrust*`.
5. THE Chat_Feature SHALL send and receive chat events only over the existing Board_Connection of the Board_Page, and SHALL add no HTTP route, no WebSocket path and no network request to any other origin.
6. IF an exception is thrown while the Poker_Chat_Handler or the Retro_Chat_Handler handles a chat event, THEN THE Poker_Chat_Handler and the Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL log only the exception's error name together with the fields permitted by criterion 2, and SHALL leave out the exception message and every payload value.
7. THE Chat_Feature SHALL write to `sessionStorage` only the Read_Marker defined in Requirement 2, holding a Sequence_Number and no other value, and SHALL write no chat key to `localStorage`.

### Requirement 20: Performance and non-regression

**User Story:** As a Participant, I want the chat to feel instant and to leave voting and the retro board exactly as responsive as before, so that adding chat costs nothing.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHILE a Board_Session of either Board_Kind has 50 open Board_Connections on a single server process, THE Poker_Chat_Handler and the Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL deliver each accepted Chat_Message to every open Board_Connection of that Board_Session, including the sending Board_Connection, within 500 ms of the server receiving the send event, for each of 20 consecutive accepted Chat_Messages sent from distinct authors, measured in a local integration test from the server's receipt of the send event to each client's receipt of the added event.
2. THE Chat_Buffer SHALL add a Chat_Message, and when it holds `CHAT_HISTORY_LIMIT` Chat_Messages evict the Chat_Message with the lowest Sequence_Number, in constant time, with no dependency on the number of Chat_Messages held, while keeping the remaining Chat_Messages in ascending Sequence_Number order.
3. THE Message_List SHALL render each Chat_Message with Angular `@for` tracked by `id`, so that adding a Chat_Message creates DOM nodes for that Chat_Message only, evicting a Chat_Message removes DOM nodes for that Chat_Message only, and the DOM nodes of every other rendered Chat_Message keep their identity.
4. THE Chat_Feature SHALL load the Chat_Panel implementation (the Chat_Panel, Panel_Header, Message_List, Composer and Presence_List components) in the Deferred chat chunk through the Panel_Chunk_Loader. The Deferred chat chunk SHALL be a separate file emitted by the Angular application builder and excluded from the initial bundle. The Panel_Chunk_Loader SHALL fetch it on the first activation of the Chat_Launcher or, where the browser defines `requestIdleCallback`, when that callback reports idle, whichever happens first. Every module that the Deferred chat chunk imports statically SHALL belong to the initial bundle or to a chunk that each Board_Page has loaded before it renders the Chat_Launcher, so that a retry fetches no file other than the Deferred chat chunk. The production build of `client/` SHALL report no `initial` budget warning (500 kB) or error (1 MB) in `client/angular.json` beyond those reported without the Chat_Feature.
5. THE Chat_Feature SHALL keep chat state in Angular signals and SHALL bridge WebSocket events with the existing `on<T>(event)` observable of the Board_Page's WebSocket service, adding no new `BehaviorSubject`.
6. THE Chat_Feature SHALL leave the name, payload shape, handling and send order of every existing poker and retro WebSocket event unchanged, so that a Board_Connection that sends no chat event receives the same sequence of non-chat events as before the Chat_Feature, with the chat history event of Requirement 18 as the only addition. The handling of Oversized_Frames in Requirement 23 and the stale-socket handling of Requirement 25 are the only changes to how existing events are sent or received. The first applies only to frames larger than `WS_MAX_PAYLOAD_BYTES`. The second applies only to events of a Stale_Socket, so every consumer of `WebSocketService` and `RetroWebSocketService` keeps receiving the same events of the Current_Socket, in the same order, as before the Chat_Feature.
7. IF an exception is thrown while a chat event is handled on the server, THEN THE Poker_Chat_Handler and the Retro_Chat_Handler SHALL keep the Board_Connection open, broadcast nothing for that event, leave the Chat_Buffer, the Sequence_Number counter, the Dedup_Record, the Chat_Rate_Limiter state and all other Board_Session state unchanged, and continue handling later events on every Board_Connection, so that the sender's `pending` Own_Message becomes `failed` through the timeout of Requirement 8 criterion 5.
8. THE System SHALL pass every server Jest suite and every client Vitest suite that existed before the Chat_Feature, with the Chat_Feature included, except these assertions of `vote-callout.stylesheet.spec.ts`: the layer's `right: 16px` and `bottom: 16px` (R7.6, R7.7), the narrow layer's `left: 16px`, `right: 16px` and `max-width: calc(100vw - 32px)` (R8.1), the narrow near-average panel's `max-width: calc(100vw - 32px)` (R8.1), and the far entrance assertion that the 0% keyframe of `callout-slide-in` holds `translateX(calc(<n>% + var(--callout-edge-gap)))` with n of at least 100 (R5.2). The anchoring assertions SHALL be replaced by assertions of the bottom-left anchoring of Requirement 12 criterion 3: `left: 16px`, a `bottom` equal to the Bottom_Left_Base_Offset (16 CSS pixels outside the narrow minimized and narrow Chat_Load_Error_Notice cases), no `right` declaration, `align-items: flex-start`, an 8 px gap, and a narrow `max-width` of `calc(100vw - 88px)` for the layer and the near-average panel. The far entrance assertion SHALL be replaced by its mirrored form under Requirement 12 criterion 13: the 0% keyframe holds `translateX(calc(-<n>% - var(--callout-edge-gap)))` with n of at least 100 and a rotation magnitude from 6 to 15 degrees, and the 100% keyframe holds `translateX(0)` with a rotation of 0. Every other assertion of that suite, including z-index 900, `isolation: isolate`, `pointer-events`, the 40vh and 45vh height limits and the control sizes, SHALL stay unchanged and pass.
9. WHILE every one of 50 open Board_Connections of a Board_Session sends chat send events at `CHAT_RATE_LIMIT_COUNT` per `CHAT_RATE_LIMIT_WINDOW_MS`, THE System SHALL deliver the broadcast of each existing poker or retro board event (for example a vote submission or a retro card addition) to every open Board_Connection of that Board_Session within 500 ms of the server receiving the triggering event, measured in a local integration test.
10. IF an attempt of a Load_Sequence fails, because its `import()` rejects or does not settle within `CHAT_PANEL_LOAD_ATTEMPT_TIMEOUT_MS` (10,000 ms), THEN THE Panel_Chunk_Loader SHALL start the next attempt 1,000 ms after the first failed attempt, 2,000 ms after the second and 4,000 ms after the third, make at most 4 attempts per Load_Sequence (1 initial attempt and 3 automatic retries), start no attempt before its delay has elapsed, and end the Load_Sequence at the first attempt that succeeds. An attempt that settled successfully after its timeout SHALL still be used if no later attempt has succeeded yet.
11. WHILE a Load_Sequence runs, including the waits between attempts, THE Chat_Launcher SHALL expose `aria-busy="true"` and the visible text "Loading chat…", stay operable, and start no second Load_Sequence when activated again. WHEN an attempt of a Load_Sequence succeeds, THE Chat_Feature SHALL remove the busy state and set Panel_State to `open` within 100 ms.
12. THE System SHALL define in `shared/types.ts` the constants `CHAT_PANEL_LOAD_RETRY_DELAYS_MS` = `[1000, 2000, 4000]` (a read-only array) and `CHAT_PANEL_LOAD_ATTEMPT_TIMEOUT_MS` = 10000, and THE Panel_Chunk_Loader SHALL take the retry delays, the per-attempt timeout and the number of automatic retries (the array length) only from these constants.
13. WHEN the Panel_Chunk_Loader starts an attempt after any earlier attempt in the same document has failed, including the first attempt of a later Load_Sequence and the first attempt after a failed idle prefetch, THE Panel_Chunk_Loader SHALL make that attempt a Cache_Busted_Import: an `import()` of the Chunk_URL with `?retry=<n>` appended (`&retry=<n>` if the Chunk_URL already holds a query string), where n is 1 for the first Cache_Busted_Import in the document and increases by 1 for each later one, so that no URL is imported twice. The Chunk_URL SHALL be resolved from the module specifier that the Angular application builder wrote into the compiled importer function, relative to `import.meta.url` of the module that holds that function, so that it names the same emitted file with the same origin and `BASE_PATH` prefix as the initial attempt.
14. IF every attempt of a Load_Sequence fails, THEN THE Chat_Feature SHALL keep Panel_State `closed`, keep the Chat_Launcher rendered and operable, leave every Board_Page control outside the chat unaffected, and show the Chat_Load_Error_Notice. The Chat_Load_Error_Notice SHALL be placed in the position of the minimized Panel_Header under Requirement 4 criterion 9, with z-index 920. It SHALL hold the text "Chat could not be loaded." inside an element with `role="alert"`, a native `<button>` "Try again" and a native `<button>` "Dismiss", each with the target size and focus indicator of Requirement 11 criterion 11. Activating "Try again" or the Chat_Launcher SHALL hide the Chat_Load_Error_Notice and start a new Load_Sequence under criteria 10, 11 and 13. Activating "Dismiss" SHALL hide the Chat_Load_Error_Notice, move keyboard focus to the Chat_Launcher and start no Load_Sequence.
15. IF the idle prefetch of criterion 4 fails, THEN THE Panel_Chunk_Loader SHALL make no automatic retry, show no error and no Chat_Load_Error_Notice, and leave the next activation of the Chat_Launcher to start a Load_Sequence whose first attempt is a Cache_Busted_Import under criterion 13. WHEN the Chat_Launcher is activated while the idle prefetch attempt is still running, THE Panel_Chunk_Loader SHALL treat that attempt as the initial attempt of the Load_Sequence.
16. WHILE the Chat_Panel implementation is not loaded, including during a Load_Sequence and after a failed Load_Sequence, THE Chat_Store SHALL keep receiving and storing the chat events of the Board_Connection, and THE Chat_Launcher SHALL keep updating the Unread_Badge, the Unread_Ring and the New_Message_Attention_Animation under Requirement 2 and Requirement 24, so that WHEN Panel_State becomes `open` after a retry, the Message_List shows every Chat_Message received in the meantime. The Chat_Store, the Chat_Launcher and the Panel_Chunk_Loader SHALL be loaded together with the Board_Page and outside the Deferred chat chunk.
17. WHEN an attempt has loaded the Deferred chat chunk, THE Panel_Chunk_Loader SHALL keep the loaded Chat_Panel implementation for the life of the document, make no further request for the Deferred chat chunk, and THE Chat_Feature SHALL open the Chat_Panel on each later activation of the Chat_Launcher with no busy state.
18. IF the Panel_Chunk_Loader cannot read a module specifier from the compiled importer function (for example in a non-bundled test environment), THEN THE Panel_Chunk_Loader SHALL make each retry by calling the importer function unchanged, keep the timing, busy state and Chat_Load_Error_Notice of criteria 10, 11 and 14, and raise no script error. Requirement 22 criterion 19 checks that every production build allows the specifier to be read.

### Requirement 21: Deployment behind a base path

**User Story:** As an operator running the app in Docker or Kubernetes under a URL prefix, I want chat to work without extra configuration, so that deployments stay unchanged.

### Requirement 23: WebSocket transport hardening

**User Story:** As an operator, I want the WebSocket servers to keep tokens out of the logs and to refuse oversized frames safely, so that chat and the boards cannot leak credentials or be knocked over by one large message.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN `server.ts` receives a WebSocket upgrade request, THE System SHALL write exactly one Upgrade_Log_Line for that request. The line SHALL hold only the part of the request URL before the first `?`: the whole URL when it has no `?`, and the empty string when the request has no URL. The line SHALL therefore hold no query string and no `token` or `sessionId` value. This applies to every upgrade request, including requests that a handler later closes with code 4001, 4004 or 4009.
2. THE System SHALL write none of the following to any server log line: a JWT, the value of a `token` query parameter, a request URL that includes its query string, or an `Authorization` header value. This applies to valid, invalid, expired and missing tokens alike, and covers the Upgrade_Log_Line, every routing, connection, close and error log line of either Board_Kind (including the log line of criterion 8), and every chat log line of Requirement 19.
3. WHEN `server.ts` routes a WebSocket upgrade request, THE System SHALL apply the routing rule in force before this feature. The routing prefix is the `BASE_PATH` value with trailing slashes removed, and is empty when `BASE_PATH` is unset, empty or `/`; `server.ts` holds this derived value in its internal `PUBLIC_PATH` constant. The System SHALL remove the prefix from the start of the request URL before matching only when `PRESERVE_PATH` is the exact string `true`, the prefix is non-empty and the request URL starts with the prefix. It SHALL then send a path starting with `/retro` to `retroWss` and every other path to the poker `wss`, and SHALL pass the request on to the connection handler with its full, unmodified URL, query string included.
4. THE System SHALL define in `shared/types.ts` the constants `WS_MAX_PAYLOAD_BYTES` = 1048576 and `WS_PAYLOAD_TOO_LARGE_CLOSE_LIMIT` = 3. It SHALL create both the poker `wss` and `retroWss` with the WS_Max_Payload set to `WS_MAX_PAYLOAD_BYTES` and SHALL leave every other `WebSocketServer` option unchanged, including `noServer: true`.
5. IF a client sends an Oversized_Frame on a Board_Connection of either Board_Kind, THEN THE System SHALL close that Board_Connection with WebSocket close code 1009. An Oversized_Frame is a message whose payload is 1,048,577 bytes or more, counting the combined payload of all fragments of one message. For that message the System SHALL run no `message` listener of `handler.ts` or `retro-handler.ts`, SHALL change no Board_Session state (participants, votes, issues, retro cards and Chat_Buffer included), and SHALL broadcast nothing.
6. WHEN a client sends a WebSocket message of at most `WS_MAX_PAYLOAD_BYTES` bytes, including a message of exactly 1,048,576 bytes, THE System SHALL deliver it to the `message` listener of its handler and process it exactly as before this feature. This includes a poker `issue:add` frame of 200 titles, each of 500 UTF-16 code units encoded as 3 bytes of UTF-8 per character, which SHALL be accepted and SHALL cause `issue:list-updated` to be broadcast.
7. THE System SHALL attach an `error` listener to every WebSocket accepted by the poker `wss` or by `retroWss` as soon as the upgrade completes, before that WebSocket is handed to `handleWebSocket` or `handleRetroWebSocket`. This includes WebSockets that those handlers then close with code 4001, 4004 or 4009. As a result, an Oversized_Frame or any other protocol error SHALL close only the affected Board_Connection, the server process SHALL keep running with no `uncaughtException`, and every other Board_Connection of every Board_Session SHALL stay open, including other Board_Connections of the same user.
8. WHEN the `error` listener of a Board_Connection receives an error, THE System SHALL write at most one log line for that error. The line SHALL hold only the following fields: the Board_Kind; the Board_Session ID, included only if the Board_Connection is registered in the client map at that moment and never taken from the request URL; the `code` property of the error (for example `WS_ERR_UNSUPPORTED_MESSAGE_LENGTH`), omitted when the error has no `code`; and the close code of the connection (1009 for an Oversized_Frame). The line SHALL hold no frame payload, no request URL, no JWT and no error message text.
9. WHEN a Board_Connection closes with close code 1009, THE System SHALL apply the close handling used for close codes 1000 and 1006 of that Board_Kind. That handling covers removal from the client map, the liveness probe bookkeeping, and removing the Participant and broadcasting `participant:left` or `retro:participant:left` under the existing participant-leave rules.
10. IF `WebSocketService` or `RetroWebSocketService` is asked to send a frame while its socket is `OPEN`, and the UTF-8 byte length of the serialised frame (the whole message, including `event`, `data` and `timestamp`) exceeds `WS_MAX_PAYLOAD_BYTES`, THEN the Client_Frame_Guard SHALL send nothing and keep the Board_Connection open. It SHALL leave the Connection_State and the count of consecutive 1009 closes unchanged. It SHALL show exactly one error notification with the text "This change is too large to send." for each rejected send call, and that notification SHALL not be dismissed by a later Connection_State change. It SHALL write no frame content to the browser console. A frame of exactly `WS_MAX_PAYLOAD_BYTES` bytes SHALL be sent, and while the socket is not `OPEN`, `send()` SHALL keep dropping every frame silently as before.
11. WHEN a Board_Connection closes with close code 1009 and the count of consecutive 1009 closes, including this close, is below `WS_PAYLOAD_TOO_LARGE_CLOSE_LIMIT`, THE Connection_Episode_Reducer SHALL start or continue a reconnect episode. The episode SHALL use Connection_State `reconnecting`, the existing backoff of `min(2^attempt × 1000, 30000)` ms and no notification. THE Connection_Episode_Reducer SHALL keep the count of consecutive 1009 closes across successful `open` events, even though `open` resets the attempt counter.
12. IF a close with code 1009 makes the count of consecutive 1009 closes of a Board_Connection reach `WS_PAYLOAD_TOO_LARGE_CLOSE_LIMIT` (3), THEN THE Connection_Episode_Reducer SHALL end the episode with Connection_State `disconnected`, after which the client WebSocket service SHALL schedule no reconnection attempt and open no new socket. THE Connection_Episode_Reducer SHALL emit exactly one error notification with the text "The connection was closed because a message was too large. Reload the page to reconnect." and SHALL emit no navigation, so the Board_Page stays displayed and the Chat_Panel shows the offline state of Requirement 9 criteria 1 and 2. Any later close event in the same tab SHALL produce no further notification until a new connection is started under criterion 13.
13. WHEN any of the following happens, THE Connection_Episode_Reducer SHALL reset the count of consecutive 1009 closes to 0: a Board_Connection closes with a code other than 1009 (including 1000, 1006, 4009, 4010 and 4004); the client WebSocket service starts a new connection through `connect()`, to the same or a different Board_Session; or the service is disconnected manually, including when the Board_Page is destroyed and when `retro:session:ended` arrives. A close that the service reports as a manual disconnect SHALL not be counted. THE Connection_Episode_Reducer SHALL keep the existing behaviour for the reserved close codes 4009, 4010 and 4004 and the existing give-up threshold of 10 attempts, together with the navigation to `/login`, unchanged.
14. FOR ALL sequences of socket events fed to the Connection_Episode_Reducer, the number of reconnection attempts scheduled between the first and the last close of a run of consecutive 1009 closes SHALL be at most `WS_PAYLOAD_TOO_LARGE_CLOSE_LIMIT` − 1, and the number of attempts within any episode SHALL be at most the existing give-up threshold of 10 (bounded-reconnect invariant property).
15. THE System SHALL confine the changes of this requirement to `server/src/server.ts`, the shared constants in `shared/types.ts`, `client/src/app/services/connection-episode.ts`, `WebSocketService`, `RetroWebSocketService` and `IssueListPanelComponent` (criterion 16 only). The stale-socket handling and the Connection_Generation of Requirement 25 are also permitted in `WebSocketService` and `RetroWebSocketService`, and are the only other change to those two services. The poker and retro handlers and their event routing SHALL stay unchanged, apart from the `error` listener of criterion 7 and the client-map lookup of the Board_Session ID for the log line of criterion 8.
16. IF the Client_Frame_Guard rejects the `issue:add` frame sent by `IssueListPanelComponent.bulkImport()`, THEN `IssueListPanelComponent` SHALL keep the Bulk_Import_Text unchanged and keep the Bulk_Import_Panel open, so that the user's pasted text is not lost. To support this, `send()` of `WebSocketService` and of `RetroWebSocketService` SHALL report to its caller whether the frame was handed to the socket (for example by returning a boolean), with no change to the behaviour of existing callers that ignore the result.

WHERE the operating system reports `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, THE Chat_Launcher SHALL play no New_Message_Attention_Animation, SHALL stop within 100 ms an animation that is running when the preference changes to `reduce`, and SHALL show the Unread_Ring of criterion 5 as the only added indication, showing and hiding the Unread_Ring in a single rendered frame with no transition.
8. WHILE Panel_State is `minimized` and the Unread_Count is greater than 0, THE minimized Panel_Header SHALL show the Unread_Dot: a static filled circle from 8 to 12 CSS pixels in diameter with `aria-hidden="true"`, inside the restore control, on the same line as the Unread_Count of Requirement 2 criterion 9 and at most 8 CSS pixels from its bounding box, with a contrast ratio of at least 3:1 against the Panel_Header background.
9. THE Chat_Feature SHALL convey new messages through the numeric Unread_Badge and the Unread_Count of the minimized Panel_Header (Requirement 2 criteria 1 to 3 and 9) together with the presence of the Unread_Ring and the Unread_Dot as shapes, so that the indication does not rely on colour alone, and SHALL report the count to assistive technology only through the accessible names and descriptions of Requirement 11 criteria 2 and 3, with no live-region announcement caused by the New_Message_Attention_Animation, the Unread_Ring or the Unread_Dot.

### Requirement 25: Stale socket isolation and connection generation

**User Story:** As a Participant, I want each board tab to act only on its current connection, so that a late event from a replaced connection cannot break my connection, trigger extra reconnects or deliver out-of-date messages.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN `WebSocketService` or `RetroWebSocketService` creates a WebSocket through `connect()` or a scheduled reconnection attempt, THE service SHALL make that WebSocket its Current_Socket and, if a previous Current_Socket exists and has not yet emitted `close`, SHALL call `close()` on that previous Current_Socket before creating the new WebSocket, as it does before the Chat_Feature, after which the previous Current_Socket is a Stale_Socket.
2. WHEN `disconnect()` of `WebSocketService` or `RetroWebSocketService` is called, including through `ngOnDestroy()`, THE service SHALL call `close()` on the Current_Socket if it has not yet emitted `close`, cancel any scheduled reconnection attempt, keep the existing single `manual-disconnect` dispatch made by `disconnect()` itself, and hold no Current_Socket until the next `connect()`, so that the socket closed by `disconnect()` is a Stale_Socket.
3. IF a Stale_Socket emits `open`, THEN THE service SHALL dispatch no event to the Connection_Episode_Reducer, leave Connection_State and Connection_Generation unchanged, call `close()` on that Stale_Socket, and write no frame to that Stale_Socket through `send()`.
4. IF a Stale_Socket emits `message`, THEN THE service SHALL discard that message and deliver it to no `on<T>()` subscriber, neither at that moment nor after a later `open` of any WebSocket, including the Chat_Store, the board pages and the internal `retro:session:ended` subscription of `RetroWebSocketService`.
5. IF a Stale_Socket emits `close` with any close code, including 1000, 1006, 1009, 4004, 4009 and 4010, or emits `error`, THEN THE service SHALL keep the Current_Socket reference unchanged, dispatch neither `close` nor `manual-disconnect` to the Connection_Episode_Reducer, leave Connection_State, Connection_Generation, the attempt counter and the count of consecutive 1009 closes unchanged, leave an already scheduled reconnection attempt neither cancelled nor rescheduled, schedule no new reconnection attempt, open no WebSocket, show no notification, dismiss no notification and navigate to no route.
6. WHEN the Current_Socket emits `open`, `message`, `close` or `error`, THE service SHALL handle the event as before the Chat_Feature: `open` dispatches `open` to the Connection_Episode_Reducer; `message` is delivered to the `on<T>()` subscribers; `close` is dispatched as `manual-disconnect` when a reserved close code or the give-up threshold has already ended the episode or, for `RetroWebSocketService`, when `retro:session:ended` has arrived on the Current_Socket, and is dispatched as `close` with its close code otherwise, under Requirement 23 criteria 11 to 13; and `error` dispatches nothing and leaves the decision to the following `close`.
7. THE `WebSocketService` and the `RetroWebSocketService` SHALL each expose the Connection_Generation as a read-only Angular `Signal<number>` named `connectionGeneration`, holding a non-negative integer that starts at 0 when the service is created, increases by exactly 1 on each `open` of the Current_Socket, never decreases, and keeps its value on every other event, including `close`, `error`, a Stale_Socket `open`, a reconnection attempt, the give-up of Requirement 23 criterion 13, `connect()` to the same or a different Board_Session, and `disconnect()`; events of one service SHALL leave the `connectionGeneration` of the other service unchanged.
8. FOR ALL sequences of `connect()`, `disconnect()`, reconnection-timer expiries and `open`, `message`, `close` and `error` events addressed to the Current_Socket or to any Stale_Socket, the Connection_State, the Connection_Generation, the Connection_Episode_Reducer state, the scheduled reconnection attempts, the notifications shown, the navigations made and the messages delivered to `on<T>()` subscribers SHALL equal those produced by the same sequence with every Stale_Socket event removed, and at every point of the sequence at most one WebSocket created by the service SHALL have neither emitted `close` nor had `close()` called on it by the service (stale-socket invariant property).
9. WHEN the Chat_Store receives a Chat_History, THE Chat_Store SHALL record the value of `connectionGeneration` of the Board_Page's WebSocket service at the moment of receipt, and IF no Chat_History has been applied since the Board_Page was rendered or since Requirement 18 criterion 11 last discarded the previous Board_Session's state, THEN THE Chat_Store SHALL apply that Chat_History under Requirement 18 criterion 13 and resend no Own_Message; criteria 11 and 12 decide every later Chat_History.
10. THE `WebSocketService` and the `RetroWebSocketService` SHALL keep the signatures and behaviour of `connect()`, `disconnect()`, `on<T>()` and `connectionState` for the Current_Socket, SHALL write every frame passed to `send()` only to the Current_Socket and only while it is `OPEN`, writing nothing to any Stale_Socket and returning `false` while there is no Current_Socket or the Current_Socket is not `OPEN`, and SHALL keep the boolean result of `send()` under Requirement 23 criterion 16 and the `connectionGeneration` signal of criterion 7 as the only additions to their public surface.
11. WHEN the Chat_Store receives a Chat_History whose recorded Connection_Generation is greater than the Connection_Generation of every Chat_History applied since the Board_Page was rendered or since Requirement 18 criterion 11 last discarded state, THE Chat_Store SHALL treat it as the first Chat_History after Connection_State changes to `connected` and apply Requirement 9 criteria 4 to 7 to it exactly once, so that the reconnect merge and the resends of Requirement 9 criterion 6 run at most once per Connection_Generation.
12. IF the Chat_Store receives a Chat_History whose recorded Connection_Generation is equal to or less than the Connection_Generation of a Chat_History already applied since the Board_Page was rendered or since Requirement 18 criterion 11 last discarded state, THEN THE Chat_Store SHALL leave its Chat_Messages, Own_Messages, Delivery_States, `CHAT_ACK_TIMEOUT_MS` timers and Unread_Count unchanged and SHALL resend no Own_Message.
